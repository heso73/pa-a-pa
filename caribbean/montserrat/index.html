A plain-words guide for first-time entrepreneurs in Montserrat: what you register, what you pay and when. Figures are 2026 estimates for planning. Montserrat Customs and Revenue Service (Inland Revenue) gives you the exact amounts when you register.

Steps to start

  1. Choose your business name and check that it is free
    Search the name at the Financial Services Commission first.
  2. Register the business name, or incorporate the company, with the Financial Services Commission
    A sole trader registers the business name. A company is incorporated.
  3. Register with the Inland Revenue and get your tax code
    Self-employed people pay provisional income tax in three equal instalments.
  4. Register with the Social Security Fund
    Self-employed people choose a weekly income band and pay a percentage of it.
  5. Ask about the Micro and Small Business incentives
    A qualifying micro or small business can get up to five years of income-tax exemption and stamp-duty relief.
  6. Open a separate bank account for the business
    Keeping business money apart from household money makes your numbers clearer and your taxes easier to pay.
  7. Register as an employer and prepare monthly payments (if you hire employees)
    With employees you deduct income tax (PAYE) and Social Security and pay them by the 14th of the following month.
  8. Register for VAT (when your sales reach the threshold)
    Required when your taxable sales pass EC$300,000 a year.

Choosing a legal form

  • Sole trader (registered business): You run the business as an individual under your own name or a business name. You and the business are the same person for tax. Usually the simplest way to start. The Financial Services Commission registers the business name and quotes the current fee. Businesses registered under the Micro and Small Business Act can get up to five years of income-tax exemption.
  • Limited company: The company is its own legal person, separate from you. It costs more to set up and has more paperwork, but it can limit your personal risk. A company costs more to set up than a sole trader business. The Financial Services Commission quotes the current fees.

Taxes and contributions

  • Income tax: 5% from EC$18,000 to EC$25,000; 20% to EC$35,000; 25% to EC$45,000; 30% to EC$150,000; 40% above (tax-free amount per year).
  • Social Security (12%): Social Security gives access to a pension and other benefits. Since January 2026 self-employed people pay 12% of their declared income. It lowers your taxable income.
  • Value Added Tax (VAT): 15%. Register once your taxable sales pass this level in a year. Your filing period is set when you register.

A micro or small business registered under the Micro and Small Business Act can get up to five years of income-tax exemption and stamp-duty relief: ask when you register.

Tax calendar

  • Every month: Self-employed people pay provisional income tax in three equal instalments: the Inland Revenue gives you the dates when you register.
  • Every year: File your annual income tax return.
  • Every month: Every month: pay income tax (PAYE) and Social Security for the previous month, by the 14th.

Frequently asked questions

How much tax does a self-employed person pay in Montserrat?

On profit: 5% from EC$18,000 to EC$25,000; 20% to EC$35,000; 25% to EC$45,000; 30% to EC$150,000; 40% above (tax-free amount per year). Contributions: Social Security 12%. Example: with EC$12,000 of sales a month, about EC$4,109 a month is left after tax.

Do I need to register for VAT in Montserrat?

Value Added Tax (VAT) 15%; register from EC$300,000 in yearly sales. Register once your taxable sales pass this level in a year. Your filing period is set when you register.

Where do I register a business in Montserrat?

With Financial Services Commission (business names registry) and Montserrat Customs and Revenue Service (Inland Revenue). First steps: Choose your business name and check that it is free; Register the business name, or incorporate the company, with the Financial Services Commission; Register with the Inland Revenue and get your tax code.

How much does it cost to employ someone in Montserrat?

On top of gross pay, the employer adds: Social Security 6%. Every month: pay income tax (PAYE) and Social Security for the previous month, by the 14th.
