Starting a business in Sint Maarten: taxes, registration and steps

A plain-words guide for first-time entrepreneurs in Sint Maarten: what you register, what you pay and when. Figures are 2026 estimates for planning. Tax Administration Sint Maarten (Belastingdienst Sint Maarten) gives you the exact amounts when you register.

Steps to start

  1. Choose your business name and check that it is free
    Search the name at the Chamber of Commerce first.
  2. Get your CRIB number from the Tax Administration
    You need the CRIB number and a business CRIB declaration for the licence application.
  3. Apply for your business licence
    Apply to the Department of Economic Licenses (TEATT). It takes at least six weeks. Bars, restaurants, hotels and supermarkets also need an operational licence.
  4. Register with the Chamber of Commerce
    A sole proprietorship registers directly. A company is incorporated by a notary and then entered in the commercial register.
  5. Register for turnover tax (BBO)
    You file and pay turnover tax of 5% on your sales every month, even in a month without sales.
  6. Open a separate bank account for the business
    Keeping business money apart from household money makes your numbers clearer and your taxes easier to pay.
  7. Register as an employer with SZV and the Tax Administration (if you hire employees)
    With employees you withhold wage tax and pay the AOV, AWW, ZV and OV premiums every month.

Choosing a legal form

  • Sole proprietorship (eenmanszaak): You work in your own name. It is the simplest and cheapest way to start. Business licence NAf 312.50 for a sole proprietorship, after an NAf 150 processing fee on submission. Chamber of Commerce registration Fls 315.00 (US$175).
  • Limited company (N.V. or B.V.): A company is a separate entity from you. It costs more to set up and needs more bookkeeping. Business licence NAf 1,550 for an N.V. or B.V., plus an NAf 312.50 director licence (NAf 625 for more than one director). A civil-law notary incorporates the company before it is entered in the Chamber of Commerce.

Taxes and contributions

  • Income tax: 12.5% up to Cg 35,831, 20% up to Cg 53,747, 26.25% up to Cg 74,649, 33.75% up to Cg 111,971, 40% up to Cg 158,252, then 47.5%, minus tax credits that make the first Cg 27,107 tax-free (tax credits equal to a tax-free income of about Cg 27,107).
  • Old age and survivors insurance (AOV/AWW) (14%): A pension insurance. Self-employed people pay it on their own income, up to Cg 132,824 a year. The premium lowers your taxable income.
  • Turnover tax (BBO): charged on sales, not on profit.

Sint Maarten has no VAT. Businesses pay a 5% turnover tax (BBO) on their sales, with no deduction for what they buy.

Your estimate uses the 2025 income tax table, the latest one published by the Tax Administration, together with the 2026 pension premium ceiling. The 2026 table replaces it as soon as you check it with the Tax Administration.

Companies pay profit tax at 34.5%. The owner's own salary or dividends are not in the estimate.

Tax calendar

  • Every month: File and pay your turnover tax (BBO) by the 15th of the following month, even when you have no sales.
  • Every year: File your income tax return (Form A). The deadline was June 1 for 2025.
  • Every month: Every month: pay wage tax and the SZV premiums for the previous month.

Frequently asked questions

How much tax does a self-employed person pay in Sint Maarten?

On profit: 12.5% up to Cg 35,831, 20% up to Cg 53,747, 26.25% up to Cg 74,649, 33.75% up to Cg 111,971, 40% up to Cg 158,252, then 47.5%, minus tax credits that make the first Cg 27,107 tax-free (tax credits equal to a tax-free income of about Cg 27,107). Contributions: Old age and survivors insurance (AOV/AWW) 14% (up to Cg 11,069 a month). On sales: Turnover tax (BBO) 5%. Example: with Cg 14,000 of sales a month, about Cg 4,209 a month is left after tax.

Is there a sales tax or VAT in Sint Maarten?

No. Sint Maarten has no general sales tax. The costs to plan for are the ones in the taxes and contributions list on this page.

Where do I register a business in Sint Maarten?

With St. Maarten Chamber of Commerce & Industry and Tax Administration Sint Maarten (Belastingdienst Sint Maarten). First steps: Choose your business name and check that it is free; Get your CRIB number from the Tax Administration; Apply for your business licence.

How much does it cost to employ someone in Sint Maarten?

On top of gross pay, the employer adds: Old age insurance (AOV) 7%, Widows and orphans insurance (AWW) 0.5%, Sickness insurance (ZV) 8.3%, Accident insurance (OV) 1%. Every month: pay wage tax and the SZV premiums for the previous month.
