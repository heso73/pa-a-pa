Starting a business in Cayman Islands: taxes, registration and steps

A plain-words guide for first-time entrepreneurs in Cayman Islands: what you register, what you pay and when. Figures are 2026 estimates for planning. Department of Commerce and Investment (DCI) gives you the exact amounts when you register.

Steps to start

  1. Choose your business name and check that it is free
    Search the name at the General Registry first.
  2. Apply for your trade and business licence
    Every business needs one before it starts trading. You apply through the Department of Commerce and Investment, and renew it every year. Micro and small businesses get reduced fees.
  3. Open a separate bank account for the business
    Keeping business money apart from household money makes your numbers clearer and your taxes easier to pay.
  4. Set up pension and health insurance for your employees (if you hire employees)
    With employees you pay 5% pension contributions (matched by the employee) and at least half of each employee's health insurance premium. Non-Caymanian staff also need a work permit.

Choosing a legal form

  • Sole trader (registered business): You run the business as an individual under your own name or a business name. You and the business are the same person for tax. Usually the simplest way to start. The Department of Commerce and Investment quotes the trade and business licence fee for your activity. Micro businesses (up to four employees and CI$250,000 in yearly revenue) pay reduced fees.
  • Limited company: The company is its own legal person, separate from you. It costs more to set up and has more paperwork, but it can limit your personal risk. A company costs more to set up than a sole trader business. The General Registry quotes the current fees.

Taxes and contributions

    The Cayman Islands has no income tax, no corporate tax and no sales tax. Your main costs are the trade and business licence, import duty on goods you bring in and, with employees, pension and health insurance.

    With employees you pay at least half of each premium for their health insurance: ask an insurer for a quote, because it is not included in your estimate.

    Tax calendar

    • Every month: With employees: pay pension contributions to your registered pension plan by the 15th of the following month.
    • Every year: Renew your trade and business licence.
    • Every month: Every month: pay the pension contributions of the previous month, and keep each employee's health insurance premium paid.

    Frequently asked questions

    How much tax does a self-employed person pay in Cayman Islands?

    On profit: No personal income tax in Cayman Islands. Contributions: None required for the owner. Example: with CI$9,000 of sales a month, about CI$4,000 a month is left after tax.

    Is there a sales tax or VAT in Cayman Islands?

    No. Cayman Islands has no general sales tax. The costs to plan for are the ones in the taxes and contributions list on this page.

    Where do I register a business in Cayman Islands?

    With Cayman Islands General Registry and Department of Commerce and Investment (DCI). First steps: Choose your business name and check that it is free; Apply for your trade and business licence; Open a separate bank account for the business.

    How much does it cost to employ someone in Cayman Islands?

    On top of gross pay, the employer adds: Pension (employer 5%) 5%. Every month: pay the pension contributions of the previous month, and keep each employee's health insurance premium paid.
