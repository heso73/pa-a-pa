Starting a business in Saba: taxes, registration and steps

A plain-words guide for first-time entrepreneurs in Saba: what you register, what you pay and when. Figures are 2026 estimates for planning. Tax and Customs Administration Caribbean Netherlands (Belastingdienst/Caribisch Nederland) gives you the exact amounts when you register.

Steps to start

  1. Choose your business name and check that it is free
    Search the name at the Chamber of Commerce first.
  2. Register with the Chamber of Commerce
    Every business is entered in the commercial register. A company is incorporated through a notary first.
  3. Check the business permit for your island
    The island government (Openbaar Lichaam) can require a permit for your activity.
  4. Register with the Tax and Customs Administration
    You get your tax number and access to the online portal MijnCN, where you file returns.
  5. Open a separate bank account for the business
    Keeping business money apart from household money makes your numbers clearer and your taxes easier to pay.
  6. Register as an employer for payroll levies (if you hire employees)
    With employees you withhold wage tax and pay employer premiums every month. The owner of a company must also take a customary salary.

Choosing a legal form

  • Sole proprietorship (eenmanszaak): You work in your own name. It is the simplest and cheapest way to start. The Chamber of Commerce quotes the registration fee for your island.
  • Limited company (B.V. or N.V.): A company is a separate entity from you. It costs more to set up and needs more bookkeeping. A company is incorporated through a notary and costs more than a sole proprietorship. The company itself pays no profit tax: the owner-director pays tax on a customary salary, and dividends bear 7.5%.

Taxes and contributions

  • Income tax and premiums: 29.4% up to US$53,198 after the tax-free allowance, then 38.4%. The rate already includes the old-age (AOV), widows and orphans (AWW) and health premiums (tax-free allowance per year).
  • General expenditure tax (ABB): charged on sales, not on profit.

Saba belongs to the Netherlands as a special municipality and pays in US dollars. Income tax, old-age insurance and health premiums are one combined charge for the self-employed: 29.4% up to US$53,198 after a US$21,956 tax-free allowance, then 38.4%.

The general expenditure tax (ABB) is 4% on services and 6% on goods you make yourself. Goods you import carry the tax at import.

A company pays no profit tax. The owner-director takes a customary salary of at least US$43,912, taxed like any wage, plus 10.3% employer premiums on it. Dividends bear 7.5% dividend tax. These are not in your estimate.

Tax calendar

  • Every month: Pay the payroll levies of the previous period. The Tax Administration gives your filing dates when you register.
  • Every year: File your income tax return online in MijnCN. Filing opens in March for the previous year.
  • Every month: Every month: withhold wage tax and pay the employer premiums for the previous period.

Frequently asked questions

How much tax does a self-employed person pay in Saba?

On profit: 29.4% up to US$53,198 after the tax-free allowance, then 38.4%. The rate already includes the old-age (AOV), widows and orphans (AWW) and health premiums (tax-free allowance per year). Contributions: None required for the owner. On sales: General expenditure tax (ABB). Example: with US$9,000 of sales a month, about US$2,790 a month is left after tax.

Is there a sales tax or VAT in Saba?

No. Saba has no general sales tax. The costs to plan for are the ones in the taxes and contributions list on this page.

Where do I register a business in Saba?

With Chamber of Commerce Saba and Tax and Customs Administration Caribbean Netherlands (Belastingdienst/Caribisch Nederland). First steps: Choose your business name and check that it is free; Register with the Chamber of Commerce; Check the business permit for your island.

How much does it cost to employ someone in Saba?

On top of gross pay, the employer adds: Sickness, accident and severance premiums (ZV, OV, Cessantia) 1%, Health-care premium (employer share) 9.3%. Every month: withhold wage tax and pay the employer premiums for the previous period.
