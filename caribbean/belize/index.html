Starting a business in Belize: taxes, registration and steps

A plain-words guide for first-time entrepreneurs in Belize: what you register, what you pay and when. Figures are 2026 estimates for planning. Belize Tax Service (BTS) gives you the exact amounts when you register.

Steps to start

  1. Register your business or company
    A company is incorporated with the Belize Companies and Corporate Affairs Registry (BCCAR). A sole trader registers the business name if it is not the owner's own name.
  2. Get your trade licence
    You get it from your local authority (town or city council) before you start trading.
  3. Register with the Belize Tax Service for business tax
    Business tax is paid every month, on your total receipts.
  4. Register with the Social Security Board
    Self-employed people can register and pay 7% of their declared weekly income.
  5. Open a separate bank account for the business
    Keeping business money apart from household money makes your numbers clearer and your taxes easier to pay.
  6. Register as an employer with the Belize Tax Service and the Social Security Board (if you hire employees)
    With employees you pay income tax withheld and Social Security each month, by the 14th of the following month.
  7. Register for GST (when your sales reach the threshold)
    Required when your sales or receipts reach BZ$75,000 a year. Register with the GST Department.

Choosing a legal form

  • Self-employed or sole trader: You run the business as an individual, under your own name or a business name. You and the business are the same person. Usually the simplest way to start. You need a trade licence from your local authority. Your town or city council gives you the current fees.
  • Limited company: The company is its own legal person, separate from you. It costs more to set up and has more paperwork, but it can limit your personal risk. A company costs more to set up than a business name. The registry will quote you the current fees.

Taxes and contributions

  • Social Security (7%): Social Security gives access to a pension and other benefits. Self-employed people pay 7% of their declared weekly income, up to BZ$320 a week.
  • Business tax: charged on sales, not on profit.
  • General Sales Tax (GST): 12.5%. Apply to the GST Department. Your filing period is set when you register.

Business tax in Belize is charged on your total receipts (sales), not on your profit. No costs are deducted. It applies once your yearly sales pass BZ$75,000 (BZ$20,000 for professional services).

Tax calendar

  • Every month: Pay business tax on your total receipts for the month. The Belize Tax Service gives you the payment date when you register.
  • By March 31: File your annual tax return. The Belize Tax Service gives you the form and the date.
  • Every month: Every month: pay Social Security and income tax withheld for the previous month.

Frequently asked questions

How much tax does a self-employed person pay in Belize?

On profit: No personal income tax in Belize. Contributions: Social Security 7% (up to BZ$1,387 a month). On sales: Business tax. Example: with BZ$12,000 of sales a month, about BZ$5,393 a month is left after tax.

Do I need to register for GST in Belize?

General Sales Tax (GST) 12.5%; register from BZ$75,000 in yearly sales. Apply to the GST Department. Your filing period is set when you register.

Where do I register a business in Belize?

With Belize Companies and Corporate Affairs Registry (BCCAR) and Belize Tax Service (BTS). First steps: Register your business or company; Get your trade licence; Register with the Belize Tax Service for business tax.

How much does it cost to employ someone in Belize?

On top of gross pay, the employer adds: Social Security 6%. Every month: pay Social Security and income tax withheld for the previous month.
