Pa a Pa Caribbean

Changelog

New countries, new languages and data updates, newest first.

2026-10-04: Haiti and US Virgin Islands checked

Haiti: the fixed patente of G 1,250, G 2,500 or G 5,000 by commune group was confirmed on the DGI site, and a note now explains that a new business pays only the fixed G 5,000 in its first period. US Virgin Islands: the US$390 yearly business licence renewal was confirmed on the DLCA site; the 2026 federal-based tax figures were re-read.

2026-10-04: Trinidad and Tobago and Barbados checked

Trinidad and Tobago: the TT$90,000 personal allowance and the 16.2% National Insurance rate for 2026 were confirmed; the rate is reported to rise to 19.2% in January 2027. Barbados: the VAT threshold rise to BDS$350,000 from 1 October 2026 is reported by two sources, not yet confirmed by the tax authority.

2026-10-03: Share tools and partner kit

Share buttons, social images, QR codes, printable flyers and a four-language partner kit for organisations.

2026-10-03: US Virgin Islands

The US Virgin Islands were added. The French West Indies link to the French version of Pa a Pa.

2026-10-03: Sint Maarten, Bonaire, Sint Eustatius and Saba

Four Dutch-language territories were added in English and Dutch. Sint Maarten uses the 2025 income tax table until the 2026 table is confirmed.

2026-10-03: Dutch interface

A Dutch interface was added for Aruba, Curaçao and Suriname.

2026-10-03: French interface and Haiti

A French interface was added with Haiti.

2026-10-03: Aruba, Curaçao, Suriname and Haiti

Four countries were added in English.

2026-10-03: British territories

The Cayman Islands, British Virgin Islands, Turks and Caicos, Anguilla and Montserrat were added.

2026-10-03: Eastern Caribbean social security rates

Social security rates for Saint Lucia, Dominica, Grenada, Saint Vincent and the Grenadines, Antigua and Barbuda and Saint Kitts and Nevis were updated from official sources.

2026-10-03: Guides for search engines and AI assistants

Each country has a static guide page, with a sitemap and an llms.txt file.

2026-10-03: Spanish interface

A Spanish interface was added for the Dominican Republic, Puerto Rico and Cuba.

2026-10-02: English version

The English version launched with Jamaica, then Trinidad and Tobago, Guyana, Barbados, The Bahamas and Belize.
