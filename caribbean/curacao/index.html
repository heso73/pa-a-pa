Starting a business in Curaçao: taxes, registration and steps

A plain-words guide for first-time entrepreneurs in Curaçao: what you register, what you pay and when. Figures are 2026 estimates for planning. Tax Authority of Curaçao (Inspectie der Belastingen) gives you the exact amounts when you register.

Steps to start

  1. Choose your business name and check that it is free
    Search the name at the Chamber of Commerce first.
  2. Register with the Chamber of Commerce
    Every business registers in the commercial register. A company is incorporated through a notary.
  3. Apply for your business licence
    Most activities need a licence from the government.
  4. Register with the Tax Authority (CRIB number)
    You get your CRIB number and your turnover tax (OB) account. You file returns even before you have sales.
  5. Register with the Social Insurance Bank (SVB)
    Self-employed people pay old age insurance (AOV) and the general insurance for exceptional medical costs (AVBZ).
  6. Open a separate bank account for the business
    Keeping business money apart from household money makes your numbers clearer and your taxes easier to pay.
  7. Register your employees and prepare payroll payments (if you hire employees)
    With employees you withhold wage tax and pay social insurance premiums every month.

Choosing a legal form

  • Sole trader (registered business): You run the business as an individual under your own name or a business name. You and the business are the same person for tax. Usually the simplest way to start. The Chamber of Commerce quotes the registration fee. You also need a business licence for your activity.
  • Limited company: The company is its own legal person, separate from you. It costs more to set up and has more paperwork, but it can limit your personal risk. A company (NV or BV) costs more to set up than a sole proprietorship. The Chamber of Commerce quotes the current fees.

Taxes and contributions

  • Income tax: 9.75% up to Cg 38,225, 15% up to Cg 50,968, 23% up to Cg 76,452, 30% up to Cg 108,306, 37.5% up to Cg 159,273, then 46.5%, minus a basic tax credit of Cg 2,850 (basic tax credit of Cg 2,850 a year).
  • Old age insurance (AOV/AWW) (15%): A pension insurance. Self-employed people pay it on their own income, up to Cg 132,824 a year.
  • Exceptional medical costs (AVBZ) (2%): A general insurance for long-term care and exceptional medical costs. It is collected with your income tax return.
  • Turnover tax (OB) (OB): 6%. A small business scheme (RKO) can exempt very small sole proprietors from filing: ask the Tax Authority. You file annually if your taxable turnover is below Cg 30,000, quarterly up to Cg 75,000, and monthly above that.

Curaçao has a turnover tax (OB) of 6% on sales, similar to a VAT. Sole proprietors pay income tax on their profit, with a basic tax credit.

Companies pay profit tax at 15% on the first Cg 500,000 and 22% above.

Tax calendar

  • Every month: File your turnover tax (OB): annually if your yearly turnover is below Cg 30,000, quarterly up to Cg 75,000 and monthly above that.
  • Every year: File your income tax return, by April 30.
  • Every month: Every month: pay wage tax and social insurance premiums for the previous month, by the 15th.

Frequently asked questions

How much tax does a self-employed person pay in Curaçao?

On profit: 9.75% up to Cg 38,225, 15% up to Cg 50,968, 23% up to Cg 76,452, 30% up to Cg 108,306, 37.5% up to Cg 159,273, then 46.5%, minus a basic tax credit of Cg 2,850 (basic tax credit of Cg 2,850 a year). Contributions: Old age insurance (AOV/AWW) 15% (up to Cg 11,069 a month), Exceptional medical costs (AVBZ) 2% (up to Cg 50,521 a month). Example: with Cg 14,000 of sales a month, about Cg 4,635 a month is left after tax.

Do I need to register for OB in Curaçao?

Turnover tax (OB) (OB) 6%; from the first sale. A small business scheme (RKO) can exempt very small sole proprietors from filing: ask the Tax Authority. You file annually if your taxable turnover is below Cg 30,000, quarterly up to Cg 75,000, and monthly above that.

Where do I register a business in Curaçao?

With Curaçao Chamber of Commerce and Tax Authority of Curaçao (Inspectie der Belastingen). First steps: Choose your business name and check that it is free; Register with the Chamber of Commerce; Apply for your business licence.

How much does it cost to employ someone in Curaçao?

On top of gross pay, the employer adds: Old age insurance (AOV) 9%, Exceptional medical costs (AVBZ) 2%. Every month: pay wage tax and social insurance premiums for the previous month, by the 15th.
