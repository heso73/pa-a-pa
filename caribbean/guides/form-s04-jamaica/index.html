Form S04 and quarterly payments for the self-employed in Jamaica

Self-employed people in Jamaica make quarterly payments and file one annual return that covers income tax, Education Tax, NHT and NIS. Here is how it works.

In short
  • S04 is the annual return of income, taxes and contributions for the self-employed. It combines income tax, Education Tax, NHT and NIS.
  • Payments are quarterly: March 15, June 15, September 15 and December 15.
  • TAJ publishes the forms and the current due dates; confirm them before you pay.

What the S04 covers

One return, four amounts: income tax on your profit, Education Tax, NHT and NIS. You do not file them separately.

Quarterly payments

You pay in four instalments during the year (March 15, June 15, September 15 and December 15), using the quarterly payment form (S03) or TAJ’s online payment options. Setting money aside each time you are paid keeps these dates from becoming a problem.

Before you file

  • Have your TRN. See how to register.
  • Work out your profit: sales minus costs. See fixed and variable costs.
  • Estimate each amount: NIS, Education Tax, NHT and income tax.

An incentive for employers

A self-employed person who employs others and pays on time can claim an Employment Tax Credit of up to 30% of the trader’s income. Ask TAJ for the current conditions.

Frequently asked questions

What is form S04 in Jamaica?

The annual return of income, taxes and contributions for self-employed people. It covers income tax, Education Tax, NHT and NIS in one filing.

When do the self-employed pay tax in Jamaica?

In four quarterly payments: March 15, June 15, September 15 and December 15.

Where do I get the forms?

From Tax Administration Jamaica (TAJ). Always use the current version.
