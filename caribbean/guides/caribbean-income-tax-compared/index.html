Income tax in the Caribbean compared

Personal income tax differs a lot across the Caribbean. This table puts the yearly tax-free amount and the main rates side by side for the countries we have checked, with a link to each country guide for the full picture.

In short
  • Rates and allowances are for personal income tax; contributions and sales taxes are on each country page.
  • Each amount is in the local currency of that country.
  • Several territories have no personal income tax or are not yet verified: see the country guides.

Frequently asked questions

Which Caribbean countries have no personal income tax?

Several territories do not charge personal income tax, and some are not yet verified here. Check the country guide for the one you are interested in.

Is the tax-free amount the same as the standard deduction?

No. The tax-free amount is the part of your yearly income that is not taxed. Each country defines its own allowances and deductions: see its guide.
