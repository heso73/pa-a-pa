Turnover tax (TOT) and business registration in Sint Maarten

Sint Maarten charges a turnover tax (TOT) of 5% on business turnover. You register with the Tax Administration, the Inspectorate of Taxes, and file monthly returns.

In short
  • The turnover tax was raised from 3% to 5% on 11 February 2011, according to the Inspectorate of Taxes. Some exemptions apply.
  • Businesses register with the Tax Administration and get a CRIB number, which the business licence application requires.
  • Wage tax, social premiums and turnover tax are filed with monthly returns. Late filing or payment can lead to an assessment with a penalty.
  • The business registration form asks for your estimated monthly turnover and gross salary.

The tax

The Inspectorate of Taxes states that the turnover tax was increased from 3% to 5% on 11 February 2011. In some cases an exemption may apply: the Inspectorate publishes a list of exemptions.

Steps

  1. Register your business with the Tax Administration, Inspectorate unit, using the Business Registration Form.
  2. Get your CRIB number. A copy of the CRIB declaration is part of the checklist for the business and director licence at the Department of Economic Licenses.
  3. Register at the Chamber of Commerce, and apply for your business licence.
  4. File the monthly return for turnover tax, and for wage tax and premiums if you have employees.

Penalties

The Tax Administration says an assessment increased with a penalty can be imposed when monthly returns are not filed on time, are incomplete or unsigned, or when the corresponding tax is not paid on time.

Out-of-island suppliers

A company doing business for the government of Sint Maarten from elsewhere is assessed the 5% TOT, unless article 7 or 8 of the National Ordinance Turnover Tax 1999 applies.

Information verified on 21 November 2026 on the Government of Sint Maarten website (sintmaartengov.org): Inspectorate of Taxes notices, Business Registration Form and the Tax Administration guide.

Frequently asked questions

What is the turnover tax rate in Sint Maarten?

5%, since 11 February 2011, according to the Inspectorate of Taxes.

Where do I register my business for tax?

With the Tax Administration, Inspectorate unit, using the Business Registration Form.

What is a CRIB number?

The tax registration number issued by the Inspectorate of Taxes, needed for the business licence.

How often do I file?

Monthly, for turnover tax and for wage tax and premiums.

What happens if I file late?

An assessment increased with a penalty can be imposed.
