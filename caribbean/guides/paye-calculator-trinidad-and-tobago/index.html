PAYE calculator for Trinidad and Tobago

PAYE is the income tax your employer takes from each pay. Enter your gross pay for one period and see the income tax, your National Insurance, the Health Surcharge and what reaches your bank account.

In short
  • Income tax: 25% on chargeable income up to TT$1,000,000 a year, then 30%, after a TT$90,000 personal allowance.
  • National Insurance: 16.2% in total in 2026, of which the employee pays 5.4% up to TT$13,600 a month. It rises to 19.2% (employee 6.4%) from 4 January 2027.
  • Health Surcharge: TT$8.25 a week (TT$4.80 for the lowest earners).

How the deductions are worked out

Income tax: your yearly pay minus National Insurance minus the TT$90,000 allowance is your chargeable income. 25% applies up to TT$1,000,000 and 30% above. The 2027 Budget kept the allowance at TT$90,000 for most people and raised it to TT$102,000 for qualifying pensioners. National Insurance is set by earnings class, with a ceiling of TT$13,600 a month. The calculator uses the employee share of the combined rate, which is a close estimate. Health Surcharge is TT$8.25 a week when you earn more than TT$109 a week, otherwise TT$4.80.

The employer pays its own National Insurance on top of your salary. Figures last checked on 12 November 2026 against the Ministry of Finance, the Inland Revenue Division (IRD), the National Insurance Board (NIBTT), the PwC Worldwide Tax Summaries and the 2027 Budget commentaries. Confirm the current amount with the IRD or your payroll provider before you pay.

Frequently asked questions

What is the income tax rate in Trinidad and Tobago?

25% on chargeable income up to TT$1,000,000 a year and 30% above, after a personal allowance of TT$90,000.

What is the National Insurance rate in Trinidad and Tobago in 2026?

16.2% of insurable earnings since 5 January 2026, split about two thirds employer (10.8%) and one third employee (5.4%). The ceiling is TT$13,600 a month. It rises to 19.2% from 4 January 2027.

How much is the Health Surcharge?

TT$8.25 a week for employees earning more than TT$109 a week (more than TT$469.99 a month), and TT$4.80 a week for others, according to the Inland Revenue Division.

Is this calculator payroll advice?

No. It is an estimate for planning. Confirm the exact amounts with the IRD or your payroll provider.
