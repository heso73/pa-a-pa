Register a micro or small business in Montserrat

Montserrat has a Micro and Small Business Act that sets size levels, and the Tax Administration Act requires you to register with the Comptroller of Inland Revenue.

In short
  • A micro business has annual sales not above $100,000 and net assets not above $75,000.
  • A small business has annual sales above $100,000 up to $1,000,000, or net assets above $75,000 up to $500,000.
  • Anyone who has to pay tax or file a return must register with the Comptroller within 30 days after the activity starts, and get a TIN.
  • Companies can register online at cipo.fsc.ms.

These are the qualifications in section 6 of the Micro and Small Business Act. Registered businesses can be exempted from certain obligations under the Act: read section 14 or ask the Micro and Small Business Committee.

Tax registration

The Tax Administration Act says a person liable to pay tax, furnish a return or perform another obligation under a revenue law, and not yet registered, must register with the Comptroller no later than 30 days after the activity begins. You need your Tax Identification Number (TIN).

Companies

Private companies limited by shares are the most common vehicle. Company registration can be done online at cipo.fsc.ms. If you trade under a name other than your own, register the business name.
