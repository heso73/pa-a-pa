Minimum wage in Jamaica in 2026

Since 1 July 2026 the national minimum wage in Jamaica is J$17,000 for a 40-hour week. Here is the hourly rate, the overtime rates, what it comes to per month, and what a minimum-wage worker takes home.

In short
  • J$17,000 per 40-hour week, up from J$16,000, from 1 July 2026.
  • J$425 an hour; overtime J$637.50 an hour; double time J$850 an hour.
  • About J$73,667 a month before deductions, and about J$68,376 after NIS, NHT and Education Tax.

What a minimum-wage worker takes home

A worker paid J$17,000 a week earns about J$73,667 a month. That is below the monthly income tax threshold, so no PAYE is due, but NIS (3%), NHT (2%) and Education Tax (2.25%) are still deducted: about J$5,291 in total. The worker keeps about J$68,376 a month.

Frequently asked questions

What is the minimum wage in Jamaica in 2026?

J$17,000 per 40-hour work week from 1 July 2026, which is J$425 an hour, according to the Jamaica Information Service.

What is the overtime rate at the minimum wage?

J$637.50 an hour for hours beyond 40 in a week, and J$850 an hour for double time, from 1 July 2026.

How much is the minimum wage per month in Jamaica?

About J$73,667 a month before deductions, working 52 weeks of 40 hours divided by 12.

Does a minimum-wage worker pay income tax?

No. The pay is below the monthly tax-free amount of J$158,530. The worker still pays NIS, NHT and Education Tax.
