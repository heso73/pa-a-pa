How to register for VAT in Saint Lucia

Saint Lucia charges VAT at 12.5%. You register with the VAT Section of the Inland Revenue Department (IRD).

In short
  • The IRD gives a standard rate of 12.5%, with a 10% reduced rate for hotel accommodation and for food, beverage and related tourism services.
  • Businesses must register within 10 days once their taxable supplies reach the threshold. There is no fee, and processing takes up to 15 working days.
  • All registered persons file a VAT return each month, due within 21 days after the end of the period.
  • Official pages disagree on the threshold: the IRD FAQ says EC$400,000, an older Government page says EC$180,000. Confirm with the IRD.

Who must register

Businesses trading in taxable supplies must register with the VAT Section of the IRD within ten days of reaching the threshold. The Government service page says there is no fee and that processing takes within 15 working days of receipt.

The threshold: two official figures

The IRD FAQ states that the threshold for Saint Lucia is $400,000 of taxable supplies in one year. An older Government of Saint Lucia service page gives $180,000. Because they differ, ask the IRD VAT Section which level applies to you: Manoel Street, Castries, tel. (758) 468-2800.

Rates

The standard rate is 12.5%. A reduced rate of 10% applies to hotel accommodation by a hotel and to food, beverage and related services, including tours, by other providers in the tourism sector.

Filing

The tax period is a calendar month. All registered persons must file a VAT return each month, and the return and payment are due within 21 days after the end of the period, according to the IRD filing guide.

Information verified on 21 November 2026 on official Saint Lucia pages: Inland Revenue Department FAQ (ird.gov.lc), Government VAT registration service page and VAT return filing guide (govt.lc). The threshold differs between official pages: confirm with the IRD.

What is the VAT rate in Saint Lucia?

12.5%, with 10% for hotel accommodation and tourism-sector food, beverage and tours.

The IRD FAQ says EC$400,000 and an older Government page says EC$180,000. Confirm with the IRD.

How fast do I have to register?

Within 10 days of reaching the threshold.

How often do I file?

Every month, with the return and payment due within 21 days after the end of the period.

Does registration cost anything?

No fee, according to the Government service page.
