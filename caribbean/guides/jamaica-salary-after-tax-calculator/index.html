Jamaica salary calculator: your take-home pay

Enter your gross monthly salary and see what is taken off for income tax (PAYE), NIS, NHT and Education Tax, and what reaches your bank account. It uses the monthly tax-free amount from Tax Administration Jamaica (TAJ).

In short
  • Employee deductions: NIS 3% (up to J$5,000,000 a year), NHT 2%, Education Tax 2.25% after NIS.
  • PAYE: 25% on pay above the monthly tax-free amount (J$158,530 from April 2026), then 30% above J$500,000 a month.
  • On J$200,000 a month, about J$23,233 is deducted and about J$176,768 reaches you.

How the deductions are worked out

NIS is 3% of your pay, up to a yearly ceiling of J$5,000,000. NHT is 2% of gross pay. Education Tax is 2.25% of your pay after NIS (and after any approved pension contribution). PAYE is 25% of what is left above the tax-free amount, and 30% on the part above J$6,000,000 a year.

Your employer also pays its own share on top of your salary: see the cost of hiring an employee in Jamaica. Frequently asked questions

How much tax do I pay on a J$150,000 monthly salary in Jamaica?

Your pay is below the monthly tax-free amount of J$158,530, so you pay no income tax. You still pay NIS (3%), NHT (2%) and Education Tax (2.25%), about J$10,774 in total, and keep about J$139,226.

What is the NHT rate for an employee in Jamaica?

2% of gross pay. The employer pays 3% on top. The employee share is refundable after seven years, according to ActivPayroll and PwC.

Is Education Tax charged on gross pay?

No. It is 2.25% of statutory income, which is your pay after NIS and approved pension contributions.

Is this calculator tax advice?

No. It is an estimate for planning. Confirm the exact amounts with TAJ or your payroll provider.
