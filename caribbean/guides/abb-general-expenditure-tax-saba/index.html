ABB (general expenditure tax) in Saba

Saba is part of Caribbean Netherlands. Since 1 January 2011 the general expenditure tax (ABB) has replaced the old turnover taxes. The Belastingdienst/Caribisch Nederland runs it.

In short
  • The ABB general rate is 6% on deliveries and imports of goods and 4% on services, and 5% on insurance.
  • It replaced the turnover tax (OB) of Bonaire and the BBO of Sint Eustatius and Saba on 1 January 2011.
  • Not every entrepreneur is liable for the ABB: you file if you receive a return form, and the Tax Administration can correct it if you were included wrongly.
  • The Belastingdienst issues return forms every quarter. You can pay monthly in advance, and file online.

The three taxable events

The ABB applies to the import of goods, the delivery of goods by a local producer, and the supply of services. For imports it is calculated on the customs value (product value plus insurance and transport). For deliveries and services it is calculated on the fee paid in money or in kind.

Filing

The ABB is a self-assessed tax: you calculate the tax and pay it regularly. The Belastingdienst/Caribisch Nederland issues return forms per quarter, so you file once per quarter, online or on paper. You can pay monthly in advance of the return. If you receive a return form, you must file, even if you had nothing to report.

How to start

Register as an entrepreneur with the Belastingdienst/Caribisch Nederland. The easiest way is to go to the tax office and ask for the returns. The Belastingdienst sends an information letter after you register during the year.

Information verified on 21 November 2026 on the Belastingdienst Caribisch Nederland website (belastingdienst-cn.nl): ABB rates, ABB handbook 2026 and the ABB return page.

Frequently asked questions

What is the ABB rate in Saba?

6% on goods, 4% on services and 5% on insurance, according to the Belastingdienst/Caribisch Nederland.

Does every business pay ABB?

No: the Belastingdienst says not every entrepreneur is liable, unlike under the old OB and BBO. You file if you receive a return form.

How often do I file?

Once per quarter, with the option to pay monthly in advance.

Can I file online?

Yes: the Belastingdienst says you can file the ABB return online.
