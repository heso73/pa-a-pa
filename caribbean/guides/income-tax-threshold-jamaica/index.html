Income tax threshold in Jamaica (2025 to 2027)

The tax-free amount is the part of your yearly income on which you pay no income tax. Jamaica is raising it in steps, each on 1 April. Because the rise starts in April, the amount that applies to a whole calendar year is slightly lower than the new yearly figure.

In short
  • From 1 April 2026: J$1,902,360 a year (J$158,530 a month).
  • Effective amount for the whole of calendar year 2026: J$1,876,614.
  • Next step on 1 April 2027: J$2,003,496 a year (J$166,958 a month).

The effective amount for a calendar year is three months at the old yearly figure and nine months at the new one. For 2026: J$449,844 plus J$1,426,770 = J$1,876,614.

Who gets it

Tax-resident individuals, employed or self-employed. Non-residents do not get the tax-free amount and pay 25% from the first dollar of Jamaican income, according to PwC.

Frequently asked questions

What is the income tax threshold in Jamaica in 2026?

J$1,902,360 a year from 1 April 2026, which is J$158,530 a month. For the whole calendar year 2026 the effective amount is J$1,876,614.

When does the threshold rise again?

On 1 April 2027, to J$2,003,496 a year, according to the TAJ schedule.

Do non-residents get the tax-free amount?

No. Non-residents pay 25% from the first dollar of Jamaican income.
