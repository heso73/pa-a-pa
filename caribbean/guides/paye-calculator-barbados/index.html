PAYE calculator for Barbados

PAYE is the income tax your employer takes from each pay. Enter your gross pay for one period and see the income tax, National Insurance, the Resilience and Regeneration Fund and what reaches your bank account.

In short
  • Income tax for 2026: 11.5% on the first BDS$50,000 above the BDS$25,000 allowance, then 27.5%. The rates were cut by one point under the 2026 Budget.
  • National Insurance: employee 11% up to BDS$5,360 a month.
  • Resilience and Regeneration Fund: 0.25% of gross pay, with no ceiling.

How the deductions are worked out

Income tax: your yearly pay minus the BDS$25,000 allowance is your taxable income. 11.5% applies to the first BDS$50,000 of it and 27.5% to the rest. The BRA says employee National Insurance contributions are not tax deductible, so the calculator takes them off after the tax. National Insurance at 11% covers the pension and related funds (health service, unemployment, training). It stops at insurable earnings of BDS$5,360 a month (BDS$1,238 a week) in 2026. The Resilience and Regeneration Fund is 0.25% of gross pay for employees and applies above the ceiling too.

The employer pays its own contributions on top of your salary. This estimate leaves out credits such as the compensatory income credit. Confirm the current amount with the BRA or your payroll provider before you pay.

Frequently asked questions

What is the income tax rate in Barbados in 2026?

11.5% on taxable income from BDS$25,001 to BDS$75,000 and 27.5% above, according to the Barbados Revenue Authority. The rates were 12.5% and 28.5% before the 2026 Budget.

What is the employee National Insurance rate in Barbados?

11% of insurable earnings up to BDS$5,360 a month in 2026, plus 0.25% to the Resilience and Regeneration Fund on gross pay.

Is National Insurance deducted before income tax in Barbados?

No. The BRA states that National Insurance paid by an employee is not tax deductible.

Is this calculator payroll advice?

No. It is an estimate for planning. Confirm the exact amounts with the BRA or your payroll provider.
