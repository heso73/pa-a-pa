How to register for VAT in Guyana

Guyana charges VAT at 14%, but only businesses registered with the Guyana Revenue Authority (GRA) can charge it. Here is when you must register and what to do first.

In short
  • You must register when your taxable activity equals or exceeds G$15,000,000 at the end of twelve months, or when you expect to pass it in the next twelve months.
  • Below the threshold you can apply for voluntary registration if you meet the GRA criteria.
  • You need a TIN (Taxpayer Identification Number) first. Sole traders can use their individual TIN for the business.
  • Failing to register can cost a penalty equal to double the VAT payable from the day you should have registered.

Who must register

The GRA says registration is mandatory once your taxable activity reaches G$15,000,000 over twelve months. If you pass it in less than twelve months, you register earlier. You must also register if you expect to exceed it during the next twelve months. The threshold has been G$15,000,000 since January 2016 (it was G$10,000,000 before).

Step 1: get your TIN

  • Apply through your GRA eServices account (register as a new taxpayer after logging in) or in person at a GRA office.
  • Sole traders do not need a company TIN: their individual TIN can be used to register the business. If you have none, bring a copy of the business registration and your ID.
  • Companies submit a TIN application signed by the Company Secretary or directors, with the business registration and incorporation documents.

Step 2: apply for VAT registration

  1. Apply to the GRA Central Registration Unit with the documents it asks for.
  2. Once you meet the requirements, you are told and a VAT Certificate is issued.
  3. Display the certificate where you carry on your taxable activity. You must not charge VAT before you are registered.

After you register

Charge 14% on taxable sales, keep invoices, and file your VAT returns monthly. Tell the GRA in writing about changes to your business details within 15 days.

Information verified on 21 November 2026 on the Guyana Revenue Authority (gra.gov.gy) VAT registration and TIN pages. Rules and forms can change: confirm with the GRA before you apply.

Frequently asked questions

What is the VAT registration threshold in Guyana?

G$15,000,000 of taxable activity over twelve months, in force since January 2016.

What is the VAT rate in Guyana?

The standard rate is 14%.

Can I register below the threshold?

Yes, voluntary registration is possible if you meet the criteria set by the GRA.

What if I forget to register?

A person who fails to register is liable to a penalty equal to double the output tax payable from the time registration was required.

Do I need a TIN before VAT?

Yes. The TIN comes first, and sole traders can use their individual TIN.
