Omzetbelasting (OB) in Curaçao: how to register and when to file

Curaçao charges a turnover tax called OB, at 6% for most sales. It works like a VAT: you charge it, then pay it to the Tax Authority.

In short
  • The standard OB rate is 6%. Some items, such as insurance and short-stay lodging, are taxed at 7%.
  • Your tax number is the CRIB (persoonsnummer), issued by the Tax Authority. You also need your Chamber of Commerce (KVK) registration.
  • Filing frequency depends on yearly taxable turnover: yearly under Cg 30,000, quarterly up to Cg 75,000, monthly above that.
  • The government has planned a simplified sales tax system for 2026: check for news before you decide.

What you need first

  • A registration at the Chamber of Commerce (KVK) for your business.
  • A CRIB number from the Tax Authority. If you do not have one, residents can also ask at the customs office (Douane) at Nieuwe Haven.

Steps

  1. Register your business at the KVK.
  2. Register with the Tax Authority (Belastingdienst Curaçao) for OB and get your CRIB number.
  3. Write your CRIB number and KVK number on every invoice you issue.
  4. Charge OB at the right rate and keep your invoices.
  5. File your OB return at the rhythm the Tax Authority assigns you (yearly, quarterly or monthly) and pay with it.

What to watch

A small business scheme can exempt very small sole proprietors from filing: ask the Tax Authority. The Tax Authority published a practical guide for new entrepreneurs in August 2026, and the government has talked about replacing OB with a simpler system.

Information verified on 21 November 2026 from the Belastingdienst Curaçao entrepreneurs guide, news coverage and tax summaries. Rates and filing rules can change: confirm with the Tax Authority.

Frequently asked questions

What is the OB rate in Curaçao?

6% standard, with some listed items at 7% or 9%.

What is a CRIB number?

Curaçao's tax identification number (persoonsnummer), issued by the Tax Authority.

How often do I file OB?

It depends on your yearly taxable turnover: yearly under Cg 30,000, quarterly up to Cg 75,000, monthly above that.

Do I have to register if I am very small?

A small business scheme can exempt very small sole proprietors from filing. Ask the Tax Authority.

Is OB going to change?

A simplified sales tax system was announced for 2026. Check the Tax Authority before you rely on today's rules.
