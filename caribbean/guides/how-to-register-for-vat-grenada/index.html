How to register for VAT in Grenada

Grenada charges VAT under the Value Added Tax Act 23 of 2009. The Inland Revenue Division (IRD) administers it.

In short
  • The IRD says VAT is charged at 15% on most goods and services, with 10% for hotel accommodation and dive operations, 20% on cell phone talk and text, and 0% on some items.
  • Businesses with annual taxable supplies above EC$300,000 must register. Promoters, licensees and proprietors of public entertainment must register whatever their turnover.
  • The IRD says VAT is payable by the 20th of the following month, and the return is lodged no later than 20 days after the end of the period.
  • Only businesses registered with the IRD can charge VAT, and they must display the original registration certificate.

Who must register

The IRD states that businesses with annual taxable supplies in excess of $300,000 are required to register for VAT. Promoters, licensees and proprietors of public entertainment must register regardless of turnover.

Rates

CaseRate
Most goods and services15%

Some goods and services0%

Filing

The IRD page says the tax is due at the end of every month and payable to the IRD by the 20th of the following month. A taxable person lodges a return for each tax period no later than 20 days after the end of the period.

How to register

The IRD page does not describe the steps. Call the Inland Revenue Division on +1 (473) 440-3556 or visit the Financial Complex, The Carenage, St. George's. Display the original VAT registration certificate in a prominent place once you are registered.

Information verified on 21 November 2026 on the Inland Revenue Division website (ird.gd, Value Added Tax page, Act 23 of 2009).
