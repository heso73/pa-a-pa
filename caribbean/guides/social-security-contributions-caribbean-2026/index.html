Social security contributions in the Caribbean in 2026

Employees and employers pay into a national scheme in every Caribbean country, at very different rates and ceilings. Here are the 2026 rates for the four countries Pa a Pa covers in detail.

In short
  • Jamaica (NIS): employee 3%, employer 3%, on pay up to J$5,000,000 a year.
  • Trinidad and Tobago (NIS): employee 5.4%, employer 10.8% in 2026 (6.4% and 12.8% from 4 January 2027), up to TT$13,600 a month.
  • Barbados (NIS): employee 11%, employer 12.75%, up to BDS$5,360 a month, plus a 0.25% fund.
  • Dominican Republic (TSS): employee 3.04% health plus 2.87% pensions; the employer pays more, with a separate monthly ceiling for each fund.

How to use these rates

Contribution = pay (up to the ceiling) x rate. The country calculators give the exact amount for your pay.

Frequently asked questions

What is the NIS rate in the Caribbean?

It depends on the country: 3% for employees in Jamaica, 5.4% in Trinidad and Tobago and 11% in Barbados in 2026. The Dominican Republic uses the TSS, with 3.04% health and 2.87% pensions for the employee.

Who pays more, the employer or the employee?

In all four countries the employer's share is equal to or higher than the employee's share.

Is there a ceiling on contributions?

Yes, in every country covered here, expressed per year in Jamaica and per month in the others.
