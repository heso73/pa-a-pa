Gross receipts tax and business license in the US Virgin Islands

The US Virgin Islands has no sales tax. Businesses pay a gross receipts tax to the Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR) and need a business license from the Department of Licensing and Consumer Affairs (DLCA).

In short
  • The gross receipts tax is a tax on total receipts from conducting a business in the Virgin Islands, without reduction for costs.
  • The BIR form states 4.5% on receipts above $9,000 a month for businesses with yearly gross receipts under $225,000. The $9,000 monthly exemption is lost if unused in a month.
  • Businesses with gross receipts above $225,000 a year file monthly, within 30 days after the end of the month. Smaller ones file an annual return within 30 days after the year.
  • A business license requires a tax clearance letter from the BIR, a police background check and a planning review.

The gross receipts tax

The BIR says the Virgin Islands gross receipts tax applies to total receipts from the conduct of business within the Territory, without reduction for expenses. The monthly return form (720 V.I.) shows a tax rate of 4.5%. Businesses with yearly gross receipts under $225,000 pay on receipts above $9,000 each month.

You can also file gross receipts online on the BIR website. The BIR annual form shows a 5% column in one place, so confirm the rate that applies to your activity with the BIR.

Business license (DLCA)

  1. Complete the Application for a U.S. Virgin Islands Business License.
  2. Get a tax clearance letter from the Bureau of Internal Revenue (good standing).
  3. The DLCA arranges a police background check on your behalf.
  4. Your business goes through a review by the Department of Planning and Natural Resources, and other reviews depending on the activity.

Information verified on 21 November 2026 on official Virgin Islands pages: Bureau of Internal Revenue (bir.vi.gov: gross receipts forms and tax booklet) and DLCA (dlca.vi.gov: steps for obtaining a license).

Frequently asked questions

Is there a sales tax in the US Virgin Islands?

The BIR tax system lists income, gross receipts, excise, hotel room, real property and other taxes. Businesses pay the gross receipts tax.

What is the gross receipts tax rate?

The BIR monthly form shows 4.5%, with a $9,000 monthly exemption for businesses under $225,000 a year. Confirm with the BIR.

How often do I file?

Monthly above $225,000 of yearly gross receipts, otherwise annually.

What do I need for a business license?

The application, a BIR tax clearance letter, a police background check and the required reviews, according to the DLCA.
