How to get a TRN in Jamaica

The Taxpayer Registration Number (TRN) is a nine-digit number that identifies you to the tax office. You need it for almost every step of starting a business, so it is the first thing to get.

In short
  • The TRN is issued by Tax Administration Jamaica (TAJ).
  • You need it before you can file returns, register for GCT or pay NHT as a self-employed person.
  • For a sole trader, your personal TRN is usually the one used for the business.

What you need to apply

  • A completed and signed TRN application form from TAJ.
  • A valid photo ID such as a passport or driver's licence.
  • Proof of your address, such as a utility bill.

Where to apply

Apply through the TAJ website or at a TAJ office. Requirements can change, so check the current list on jamaicatax.gov.jm or call 888-TAX-HELP (888-829-4357).

Companies and sole traders

A sole trader registers a business name at the Companies Office of Jamaica (COJ) and normally uses a personal TRN. A limited company must register with the COJ first and then receives its own TRN. See the cost to register a business.

What comes next

Once you have a TRN you can register for the taxes that apply to you: income tax, NHT, NIS and, above J$15,000,000 of yearly sales, GCT. Frequently asked questions

What is a TRN in Jamaica?

The Taxpayer Registration Number, a nine-digit number issued by Tax Administration Jamaica to identify every taxpayer.

Do I need a TRN to start a business in Jamaica?

Yes. You need it for almost every registration step.

Does a sole trader need a separate TRN?

Usually not. A sole trader normally uses their personal TRN. A limited company gets its own.
