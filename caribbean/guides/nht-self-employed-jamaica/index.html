NHT contributions for the self-employed in Jamaica

The National Housing Trust (NHT) helps contributors get housing loans. If you work for yourself, you contribute to it yourself, because there is no employer to do it for you.

In short
  • Most self-employed people contribute 3% of their income.
  • You register as a self-employed contributor through Tax Administration Jamaica (TAJ).
  • You pay by the 15th of March, June, September and December.

The rate

The NHT says self-employed people in categories 1 to 5 contribute 3% of their income. Some categories, such as domestic workers and local caregivers in private homes, contribute 2% of gross earnings. Check which category applies to you with the NHT or TAJ.

How to register and pay

  • Get your TRN from TAJ if you do not have one yet (see how to get a TRN).
  • Register as a self-employed contributor through the TAJ website or at the NHT compliance department in a branch office.
  • Pay at TAJ, online or in office, by the 15th of March, June, September and December.

Add it to your budget

NHT is one of four amounts that come out of your profit with income tax, NIS and Education Tax. See the calculator for the total. Frequently asked questions

What is the NHT rate for the self-employed in Jamaica?

Most self-employed people contribute 3% of their income, according to the NHT. A few categories contribute 2%.

When do I pay NHT as a self-employed person?

By the 15th of March, June, September and December, at Tax Administration Jamaica.

Do I get the money back?

NHT contributions give access to housing benefits for contributors. Ask the NHT for the exact refund and loan conditions that apply to you.
