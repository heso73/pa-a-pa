How to register for VAT in Dominica

Dominica has charged VAT since 1 March 2006. The Inland Revenue Division (IRD) administers it.

In short
  • The standard VAT rate is 15%. Accommodation and diving activity carry 10%.
  • The IRD says businesses with annual taxable supplies above EC$250,000 must register for VAT. The threshold rose to $250,000 on 1 September 2016.
  • Registered persons must display their VAT registration certificate, and prices must be quoted VAT inclusive.
  • Before you start a business, contact the IRD and complete a registration form.

Who must register

According to the IRD FAQ, businesses with annual taxable supplies in excess of $250,000 are required to register for VAT. The IRD announced on 12 September 2016 that the threshold had increased to $250,000, from $60,000 and $120,000.

Rates

Goods and services15%
Hotel accommodation and dive activity10%

Steps

  1. Contact the Inland Revenue Division before you start operating.
  2. Complete the business registration form. The IRD will tell you whether you need a licence.
  3. If your taxable supplies pass $250,000 a year, register for VAT. The IRD publishes a VAT Registration Guide (2018).
  4. Display your VAT registration certificate and show prices VAT inclusive.

Information verified on 21 November 2026 on the Inland Revenue Division website (ird.gov.dm: VAT page, FAQ, threshold notice and Starting a Business page).

Frequently asked questions

What is the VAT rate in Dominica?

15%, with 10% for hotel accommodation and dive activity.

What is the VAT registration threshold?

EC$250,000 of annual taxable supplies, according to the IRD.

Must I show prices with VAT?

Yes: the IRD says prices quoted, advertised or displayed must be VAT inclusive.

Where do I start?

Contact the Inland Revenue Division and complete the registration form.
