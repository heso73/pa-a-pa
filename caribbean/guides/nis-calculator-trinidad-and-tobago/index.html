National Insurance (NIS) calculator for Trinidad and Tobago

National Insurance gives access to a pension and other benefits. Enter a monthly pay and see the contribution for an employee or an employer, in 2026 and from January 2027.

In short
  • 2026: 16.2% in total, employee 5.4% and employer 10.8%.
  • From 4 January 2027: 19.2% in total, employee 6.4% and employer 12.8%.
  • Contributions stop at insurable earnings of TT$13,600 a month. The highest weekly total is TT$508.50 in 2026 and TT$602.40 in 2027.

Frequently asked questions

What is the National Insurance rate in Trinidad and Tobago?

16.2% of insurable earnings since 5 January 2026, about two thirds paid by the employer and one third by the employee. It rises to 19.2% from 4 January 2027.

What is the National Insurance ceiling?

Insurable earnings stop at TT$13,600 a month, which is TT$3,138 a week.

Does the self-employed person pay National Insurance?

The NIBTT FAQ says the scheme does not yet cover the self-employed, while some payroll sites say they pay the combined rate. Ask the NIBTT before you count on it.
