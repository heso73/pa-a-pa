Business Licence and taxes in the Turks and Caicos Islands

In the Turks and Caicos Islands, the Revenue Department issues business licences and collects sales-type taxes. VAT was repealed in 2012.

In short
  • All businesses operating in and from the Islands, except those exempt by law, must hold a valid business licence.
  • Apply to the Revenue Department, which also administers business licensing.
  • There are five sales-type taxes, imposed on sectors such as tourism, financial services and telecommunications.
  • Vehicle Hire Stamp Duty is 12% and the Domestic Financial Services Sales Tax is 12%, according to the Revenue Department.

Business licence first

The Revenue Department says all businesses operating in and from the Turks and Caicos Islands, except those exempt by law, must hold a valid business licence. The business licence application is completed and turned in to the Revenue Department.

Then register for your tax stream

On receipt of the business licence certificate, taxpayers submit a registration form to the Revenue Department. Registration forms are available for the Hotel and Tourism Tax, Vehicle Hire Stamp Duty, Domestic Financial Services Sales Tax, Insurance Premium Services Sales Tax and Telecommunications Tax.

Rates we could confirm

  • Vehicle Hire Stamp Duty: 12% on the rental of motor vehicles, including motorcycles and scooters. Registration is mandatory.
  • Domestic Financial Services Sales Tax: 12% on service fees and commissions charged by banks and money transmitters on outgoing international transfers.

The Revenue Department says the VAT initiative was repealed in 2012. Ask the department for the rates of the other taxes that apply to your activity.

Information verified on 21 November 2026 on the Turks and Caicos Islands Government website (gov.tc, Revenue Department pages and busreg.gov.tc).

Frequently asked questions

Is there VAT in the Turks and Caicos Islands?

No: the Revenue Department says the VAT initiative was repealed in 2012. There are five sales-type taxes by sector.

Who issues business licences?

The Revenue Department.

Do I register for tax before or after the licence?

After: the registration form is submitted on receipt of the business licence certificate.

What is the stamp duty on vehicle hire?

12%, according to the Revenue Department.
