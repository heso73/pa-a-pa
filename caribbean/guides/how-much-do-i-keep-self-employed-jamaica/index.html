How much do you keep after tax in Jamaica?

A self-employed person pays income tax and three contributions out of profit. This table shows what is left at different yearly profit levels, using the 2026 rules. Find your row, or use the calculator for an exact figure.

In short
  • Based on 2026 rules: tax-free amount J$1,876,614, NIS 6%, NHT 3%, Education Tax 2.25%.
  • Profit means sales minus your business costs, before tax.
  • Up to about J$2 million of profit you pay almost no income tax, but you still pay the contributions.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a self-employed person keep on J$3,000,000 of profit in Jamaica?

About J$2,430,704 a year, or J$202,559 a month, after income tax, NIS, NHT and Education Tax, under 2026 rules.

Do I pay contributions if I pay no income tax?

Yes. NIS, NHT and Education Tax apply to your profit even when it is below the income tax threshold.
