Cost of hiring an employee in Jamaica

A salary is not the whole cost of an employee. As an employer you also pay four contributions on top. Together they add roughly 12.5% to the gross salary. Use the calculator to see the real monthly cost.

In short
  • Employer NIS 3% (up to J$5,000,000 a year) and NHT 3%.
  • Employer Education Tax 3.5% and HEART Trust levy 3%.
  • You deduct the employee's share from their pay and remit everything to TAJ by the 14th of the following month.

What you must do as an employer

  • Register as an employer with TAJ.
  • Deduct PAYE, the employee's NIS, NHT and Education Tax from each salary.
  • Remit these deductions and your own employer contributions by the 14th of the month after payment.

Plan before you hire

Add about 12.5% to every salary in your business test before deciding whether you can afford a first employee. The HEART levy applies once your wage bill passes the threshold set by HEART/NSTA, so confirm it for your case. Frequently asked questions

How much does an employer pay on top of salary in Jamaica?

Employer NIS 3%, NHT 3%, Education Tax 3.5% and HEART 3%, about 12.5% together, according to PwC and TAJ guidance.

When are payroll contributions due?

By the 14th day of the month after the salary is paid.

Does a sole trader need to register as an employer?

Yes, as soon as you hire. You register for PAYE and deduct and remit contributions to TAJ.
