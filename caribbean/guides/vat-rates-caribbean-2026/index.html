VAT and sales tax rates in the Caribbean in 2026

Each Caribbean country charges its own sales tax, under a different name, at a different rate and with a different registration threshold. Here are the four countries Pa a Pa covers in detail, side by side.

In short
  • Rates: Jamaica GCT 15%, Trinidad and Tobago VAT 12.5%, Barbados VAT 17.5%, Dominican Republic ITBIS 18%.
  • Registration thresholds: Jamaica J$15,000,000 of yearly sales, Trinidad and Tobago TT$500,000, Barbados BDS$350,000 from 1 October 2026 (it was BDS$200,000). The Dominican Republic has no minimum of sales.
  • To add a tax to a price, multiply by 1 plus the rate; to remove it from a total, divide by the same number.

Quick maths

Price with tax = price x 1.15 (Jamaica), x 1.125 (Trinidad and Tobago), x 1.175 (Barbados) or x 1.18 (Dominican Republic). On a price of 1,000 before tax the tax is 150, 125, 175 or 180 in the local currency.

Frequently asked questions

What is the VAT rate in the Caribbean?

There is no regional rate. It is 15% in Jamaica (GCT), 12.5% in Trinidad and Tobago, 17.5% in Barbados and 18% in the Dominican Republic (ITBIS).

Which Caribbean country has the highest VAT?

Of the four covered here, the Dominican Republic at 18%, then Barbados at 17.5%, Jamaica at 15% and Trinidad and Tobago at 12.5%.

When must a small business register for VAT in the Caribbean?

Jamaica: J$15,000,000 of yearly sales. Trinidad and Tobago: TT$500,000 in twelve months. Barbados: BDS$350,000 a year from 1 October 2026. The Dominican Republic has no sales minimum.
