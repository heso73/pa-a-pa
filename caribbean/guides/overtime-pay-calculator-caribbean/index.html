Overtime pay calculator for the Caribbean

Overtime is paid at a higher rate than your ordinary hour. Enter your hourly pay and your overtime hours and see the overtime rate and the total for the week. Below, the overtime rates at the minimum wage in three countries.

In short
  • Overtime is usually paid at 150% of the ordinary hourly rate (time and a half), and 200% on some days or in some cases.
  • Jamaica: J$637.50 an hour at the minimum wage, J$850 for double time.
  • Barbados: BDS$16.07 an hour at the minimum wage. Trinidad and Tobago: TT$30.75 an hour at the minimum wage.

How overtime is worked out

Overtime pay = ordinary hourly rate x multiplier x overtime hours. Example: at TT$20.50 an hour, 5 hours at time and a half is 20.50 x 1.5 x 5 = TT$153.75.

Frequently asked questions

How is overtime pay calculated?

Multiply your ordinary hourly rate by the overtime multiplier (1.5 for time and a half, 2 for double time) and by the number of overtime hours.

What is the overtime rate in Jamaica?

At the minimum wage, J$637.50 an hour for hours beyond 40 in a week and J$850 an hour for double time, from 1 July 2026.

What is the overtime rate in Barbados?

At least 150% of the ordinary rate, which is about BDS$16.07 an hour at the minimum wage.
