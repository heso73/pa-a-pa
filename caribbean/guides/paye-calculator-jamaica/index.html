PAYE calculator for Jamaica

PAYE is the income tax your employer takes from each pay. Choose how you are paid, enter your gross pay for one period, and see the tax-free amount, the deductions and your take-home pay.

In short
  • Tax-free amount from April 2026: J$158,530 a month, J$73,168 a fortnight, J$36,584 a week.
  • PAYE: 25% above the tax-free amount, 30% on the part above J$6,000,000 a year.
  • NIS 3%, NHT 2% and Education Tax 2.25% (after NIS) come off as well.

Frequently asked questions

What is the PAYE threshold in Jamaica per month?

J$158,530 a month from April 2026, which is J$1,902,360 a year, according to TAJ.

What is the weekly tax-free amount in Jamaica?

About J$36,584 a week from April 2026.

What is the PAYE rate in Jamaica?

25% on pay above the tax-free amount, and 30% on the part above J$6,000,000 a year.
