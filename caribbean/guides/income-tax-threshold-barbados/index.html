Income tax threshold in Barbados in 2026

The personal allowance in Barbados is BDS$25,000 a year, about BDS$2,083 a month. Below it you pay no income tax. Here are the rates above it, the tax by monthly pay, and a calculator.

In short
  • Tax-free allowance: BDS$25,000 a year (about BDS$2,083 a month).
  • Income tax: 11.5% on the first BDS$50,000 above the allowance, then 27.5%.
  • National Insurance (11% for the employee) and the 0.25% Resilience and Regeneration Fund are separate from income tax and are still charged.

What the threshold is

Your taxable income is your yearly pay minus the BDS$25,000 allowance. If it is zero or negative, no income tax is due. National Insurance paid by an employee is not tax deductible, according to the BRA, so it does not reduce taxable income.

Frequently asked questions

What is the income tax threshold in Barbados?

BDS$25,000 a year, about BDS$2,083 a month. Taxable income below it is not taxed.

What is the income tax rate in Barbados?

11.5% on the first BDS$50,000 of taxable income above the allowance, and 27.5% on the rest.

Does a minimum-wage worker pay income tax in Barbados?

No. The yearly pay of about BDS$22,277 is below the BDS$25,000 allowance. National Insurance and the Resilience and Regeneration Fund are still deducted.
