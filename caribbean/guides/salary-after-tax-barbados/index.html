Salary after tax in Barbados in 2026

What do you really keep from a gross salary in Barbados? Enter your monthly pay and see the deductions (National Insurance, Resilience and Regeneration Fund, income tax) and your take-home pay. A table below shows common salaries.

In short
  • On BDS$5,000 gross a month, about BDS$898 is deducted and BDS$4,102 reaches your bank account.
  • Deductions: employee National Insurance (11% up to BDS$5,360 a month), the 0.25% Resilience and Regeneration Fund and income tax (11.5% then 27.5% above the BDS$25,000 allowance).
  • At the minimum wage (about BDS$1,856 a month) no income tax is due.

Frequently asked questions

How much tax do I pay on BDS$5,000 a month in Barbados?

About BDS$335 a month in income tax, on top of National Insurance and the Resilience and Regeneration Fund, with the 2026 rates and the BDS$25,000 allowance.

What is the take-home pay on BDS$5,000 a month?

About BDS$4,102 a month after National Insurance, the Resilience and Regeneration Fund and income tax.

Is National Insurance tax deductible in Barbados?

No. The BRA says National Insurance paid by an employee is not tax deductible, so it does not lower taxable income.
