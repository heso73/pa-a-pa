Tax Compliance Certificate (TCC) in Jamaica: what it is and how to get one

A TCC is proof from Tax Administration Jamaica (TAJ) that your taxes and wage-related statutory payments are up to date. Many small businesses are asked for one sooner than they expect.

In short
  • The TCC shows that tax liabilities and wage-related deductions (NIS, NHT, Education Tax, HEART) are up to date.
  • It is commonly asked for to clear goods at Customs, to bid for government contracts, for certain licences, work permits and business loans.
  • You apply through TAJ, online with your TRN and eServices account or at a tax office. Companies can also get a first TCC at the Companies Office of Jamaica on registration, valid for three months.
  • TAJ can auto-renew a TCC as a Gold TCC valid for one year if you had no late returns or payments over the past year.

When you need it

  • Clearing commercial goods and motor vehicles with Jamaica Customs.
  • Government contracts: public bids above a set value ask for proof of tax compliance.
  • Certain licences, work permits and some business loans.

Before you apply

  • Have your TRN (it is free and takes minutes to get from TAJ).
  • File your returns and pay what you owe: income tax, GCT if registered, payroll taxes (PAYE, NIS, NHT, Education Tax, HEART) and any trade licence obligations.
  • Employers may be asked for clearance letters from the statutory bodies as part of the first application.

How to apply

  1. Create or log in to your TAJ eServices account (you need your TRN to register).
  2. Use the TCC application in eServices, or visit a TAJ tax office with your documents.
  3. If you are compliant, TAJ issues a Tax Compliance Letter or certificate that you can print and that others can verify online with your TRN and TCC number.

Renewal

If your TCC expired no more than a month ago and your returns and payments are in order, TAJ can renew it automatically as a Gold TCC valid for one year. If you were late with returns or payments, you apply again as normal.

After Hurricane Melissa

TAJ announced a one-time TCC with relaxed criteria for taxpayers in the seven named parishes affected. Ask TAJ if you qualify.

Frequently asked questions

How long does a TCC last?

A TCC issued by TAJ has a limited validity. A TCC issued by the Companies Office of Jamaica on registration is valid for three months. An auto-renewed Gold TCC is valid for one year.

Is there a fee?

Check the TAJ rates and fees page for the current amount before you apply.

Can I get a TCC if I owe tax?

Generally TAJ issues it when you are compliant, or have an arrangement to pay. After Hurricane Melissa, TAJ offered a one-time relaxed TCC for eligible taxpayers.

Do I need a TCC to open a business?

Not to register a name. But many customers, banks and government bodies will ask for it, so plan to be compliant from the start.

Who do I call?

TAJ Customer Care: 1-888-TAX-HELP (888-829-4357) or taxhelp@taj.gov.jm.
