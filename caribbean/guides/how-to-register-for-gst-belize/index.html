How to register for GST in Belize

Belize charges 12.5% General Sales Tax (GST). Once your yearly turnover reaches BZ$75,000 you must register with the Belize Tax Service. Here is how.

In short
  • You must register for GST when your annual turnover is BZ$75,000 or more (about BZ$6,250 a month).
  • The standard GST rate is 12.5%.
  • Once you pass the threshold, registration must be completed within one month.
  • Registered businesses file monthly returns, due by the 15th of the following month, through IRIS Belize.

Who must register

Any person, company, partnership or sole proprietor carrying on a taxable activity with annual turnover of BZ$75,000 or more must register. Turnover is the total of taxable sales, not counting the GST charged. Below the threshold you can apply for voluntary registration.

How to register

  1. Register your business and get your trade licence first.
  2. Fill in the Belize Tax Service Application for Registration form (BTS100). Individuals add form BTS101 with a copy of their social security card or passport.
  3. Submit it with supporting documents to the Belize Tax Service. Voluntary registrations use form BTS102.
  4. Create an IRIS Belize online account to file and pay.

After you register

Charge 12.5% on taxable sales (basic goods and services can be zero-rated or exempt), keep invoices, and file your GST return and payment each month by the 15th of the next month.

Information verified on 21 November 2026 on the Belize Tax Service (bts.gov.bz) registration and FAQ pages. Forms can change: confirm with the Belize Tax Service before you apply.

Frequently asked questions

What is the GST registration threshold in Belize?

BZ$75,000 of annual turnover, or about BZ$6,250 a month.

What is the GST rate in Belize?

The standard rate is 12.5%.

How long do I have to register?

Registration must be completed within one month from the date you become eligible.

Where do I file my GST return?

Through the IRIS Belize online portal, by the 15th of the month after the sales.

Can I register voluntarily?

Yes, below the threshold you can apply using form BTS102.
