VAT calculator for Barbados

Value Added Tax (VAT) in Barbados is 17.5%. Enter an amount to add the VAT to a price, or to find the price before VAT inside a total.

In short
  • Standard VAT rate: 17.5%.
  • Registration threshold: BDS$200,000 of yearly sales. The 2026 Budget raises it to BDS$350,000 from 1 October 2026.
  • Returns are filed every two months, by the 21st of the month after the period.

How it works

To add VAT, multiply the price by 1.175. To take VAT out of a total, divide it by 1.175. On BDS$1,175 the VAT is BDS$175 and the price before VAT is BDS$1,000.

The registration threshold

A business must register once its yearly sales reach the threshold. It was BDS$200,000. The Budget presented on 16 March 2026 raised it to BDS$350,000 from 1 October 2026, according to PwC, EY and KPMG. The BRA pages we checked may still show BDS$200,000, so ask the BRA which amount applies before you decide not to register. Registration is through the BRA online portal. Figures last checked on 12 November 2026 against the Barbados Revenue Authority (BRA), the National Insurance Office, the Ministry of Labour notice and the PwC Worldwide Tax Summaries. Confirm the current amount with the BRA or your payroll provider before you pay.

Remember

Some goods and services are zero-rated or exempt, for example exports and some tourism services, so 17.5% does not apply to everything. Check your item with the BRA before you charge or claim VAT.

Frequently asked questions

What is the VAT rate in Barbados?

17.5% on most goods and services, according to the Barbados Revenue Authority and PwC.

How do I take VAT out of a price?

Divide the VAT-inclusive total by 1.175. The difference is the VAT.

What is the VAT registration threshold in Barbados?

BDS$200,000 of yearly sales. The 2026 Budget raises it to BDS$350,000 from 1 October 2026.

How often are VAT returns filed in Barbados?

Every two months, with the return and payment due by the 21st of the month after the period.
