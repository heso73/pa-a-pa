How much does it cost to register a business in Jamaica?

There are two common ways to start: a registered business name, which is the simplest, or a limited company, which costs more and has more paperwork but can limit your personal risk. Here is what each costs and what you need.

In short
  • Registered business name: J$2,500, valid for 3 years.
  • Limited company: about J$30,500 (J$27,000 registration, J$500 name search, J$3,000 name reservation).
  • You need a TRN (tax number) first. Registration is done at the Companies Office of Jamaica (COJ).

Option 1: registered business name

You run the business as an individual, under your own name or a business name. For tax, you and the business are the same person. It is usually the simplest way to start.

Business name registration (valid 3 years)J$2,500

What you need: your TRN, a valid ID and a business address in Jamaica. If you trade under any name other than your own full name, you must register it. Search the name at the COJ first.

Option 2: limited company

The company is its own legal person, separate from you. It costs more to set up and has more paperwork, but it can limit your personal risk.

COJ registrationJ$27,000
Name searchJ$500
Name reservationJ$3,000
TotalJ$30,500

What you need: a name reservation, articles of incorporation, a beneficial ownership return, a TRN for the directors and the ID of the principal director.

The steps, in order

  • Get your TRN from Tax Administration Jamaica (TAJ).
  • Choose your name and check that it is free at the COJ.
  • Register with the Companies Office of Jamaica, online or in person. A business name is registered for three years, then renewed.
  • For a limited company, complete the Business Registration Form (BRF1), which also covers your tax and contribution registrations.
  • If you work for yourself, register with TAJ and file your yearly income estimate (form S04A) by March 15.

Which one should I choose?

If you start small and alone, the business name is cheaper and quicker. A company makes sense when you want to separate your personal assets from the business or take on partners. Ask the COJ or an accountant before deciding. These fees do not include professional help, such as a lawyer for the articles.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to register a business name in Jamaica?

J$2,500, valid for three years, at the Companies Office of Jamaica.

How much does it cost to register a limited company in Jamaica?

About J$30,500 in official fees: J$27,000 for COJ registration, J$500 for the name search and J$3,000 for the name reservation. Lawyer or accountant fees are extra.

Do I need a TRN before I register?

Yes. The TRN is needed for almost every step. Get it from Tax Administration Jamaica first.
