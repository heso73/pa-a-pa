Education Tax in Jamaica: rates and how it is calculated

Education Tax is one of the four payroll deductions in Jamaica. Here is who pays it, at what rate, on what amount, and a worked example in J$.

In short
  • Employee: 2.25% of statutory income. Employer: 3.5% of statutory income. Self-employed: 2.25%.
  • Statutory income is gross pay less your NIS contribution (and approved pension contributions).
  • The order is: NIS first, then Education Tax, then income tax (PAYE).
  • Employers pay the deductions by the 14th of the following month (form S01).

Who pays Education Tax

Both sides of an employment relationship pay it. The employee has 2.25% taken from pay, and the employer adds 3.5% on top. A self-employed person pays 2.25% on their own income, through the quarterly payments and the annual return.

What it is calculated on

Not on gross pay, but on statutory income: gross pay minus the employee NIS contribution (and approved pension contributions). Because NIS comes off first, Education Tax is a little lower than 2.25% of gross.

Worked example: J$120,000 a month

Gross monthly payJ$120,000
NIS at 3%J$3,600
Statutory incomeJ$116,400
Employee Education Tax at 2.25%J$2,619
Employer Education Tax at 3.5%J$4,074

The employee Education Tax is taken from the pay slip. The employer cost of J$4,074 is on top of the J$120,000, along with NIS, NHT and HEART/NTA.

If you work for yourself

Education Tax is one of the amounts on your yearly return, together with income tax, NIS and NHT. See the S04 return and quarterly payments.

A change to watch

Jamaica has announced a plan to merge the four payroll deductions into one single tax. At the last check, TAJ still described four separate deductions, so plan with the rates on this page until TAJ confirms the change.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Education Tax rate in Jamaica?

2.25% of statutory income for employees and the self-employed, and 3.5% for employers.

What is statutory income?

Gross pay less the employee NIS contribution and approved pension contributions. Education Tax is calculated on this amount, not on gross pay.

When do employers pay Education Tax?

With the other payroll deductions, by the 14th of the month after the pay period, using form S01.
