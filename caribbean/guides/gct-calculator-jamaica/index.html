GCT calculator for Jamaica

General Consumption Tax (GCT) is Jamaica's sales tax, like VAT. Enter an amount to add the 15% to a price, or to find the price before GCT inside a total.

In short
  • Standard GCT rate: 15%.
  • You must register once your yearly taxable sales pass J$15,000,000: register within 21 days.
  • GCT returns are due by the 25th of the month after the period.

How it works

To add GCT, multiply the price by 1.15. To take GCT out of a total, divide it by 1.15. On J$11,500 the GCT is J$1,500 and the price before GCT is J$10,000.

Most small businesses start below the registration threshold and do not charge GCT yet. Read when a business must register for GCT for the threshold and deadlines. Figures last checked on 10 November 2026 against the official sources (TAJ, NHT) and the PwC Worldwide Tax Summaries. Confirm the current amount with the authority before you pay.

Remember

Some goods and services are zero-rated or exempt, so the 15% does not apply to everything. Check your item with TAJ before you charge or claim GCT.

Frequently asked questions

What is the GCT rate in Jamaica?

The standard rate is 15%.

How do I take GCT out of a price?

Divide the GCT-inclusive total by 1.15. The difference is the GCT.

When must a business register for GCT in Jamaica?

When yearly taxable sales pass J$15,000,000. You then have 21 days to register.
