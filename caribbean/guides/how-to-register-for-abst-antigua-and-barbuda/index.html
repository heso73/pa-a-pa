How to register for ABST in Antigua and Barbuda

Antigua and Barbuda charges the ABST, a value added tax run by the Inland Revenue Department (IRD). There is no fee to register.

In short
  • The 2024 Budget Statement raised the ABST rate from 15% to 17% and applied it to the tourism sector, which had a 14% rate.
  • The official IRD registration form asks whether you expect taxable supplies above EC$300,000 over the next 12 months.
  • The IRD may register a business below the threshold, but registration is not guaranteed.
  • There is no registration fee for ABST.

Who must register

The IRD application form for ABST registration asks: "Do you expect taxable supplies for the next 12 months to exceed $300,000?" Answer yes if you are above the level now, or likely to pass it in the next 12 months.

Below the threshold

The Commissioner of Inland Revenue may register a person who is below the threshold. Registration is not guaranteed.

Rate

The government's 2024 Budget Statement increased the ABST rate from 15% to 17% and ended the lower tourism rate. Older IRD documents still show 15% and 14%: they predate that change.

How to register

  1. Download the ABST registration application from the IRD website (ird.gov.ag).
  2. Fill it in and answer the threshold questions.
  3. Submit it to the Inland Revenue Department in St John's. There is no fee.
  4. File your ABST return each month on the IRD form, on time: the IRD lists penalties for late filing and late payment.

Information verified on 21 November 2026 from the Inland Revenue Department (ird.gov.ag: ABST registration form and FAQ) and the Government of Antigua and Barbuda 2024 Budget Statement (ab.gov.ag).

Frequently asked questions

What is the ABST rate?

17%, after the 2024 Budget Statement raised it from 15%.

What is the registration threshold?

The IRD form asks about expected taxable supplies above EC$300,000 over the next 12 months.

Does it cost anything to register?

No: the IRD says there is no registration fee for ABST.

Can I register below the threshold?

The Commissioner may register you, but it is not guaranteed.
