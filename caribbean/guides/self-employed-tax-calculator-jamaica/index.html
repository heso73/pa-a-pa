Self-employed tax calculator for Jamaica

Enter your yearly profit and see the four things that come out of it: NIS, NHT, Education Tax and income tax. It uses the 2026 or 2027 tax-free amount from Tax Administration Jamaica (TAJ).

In short
  • NIS 6% up to J$5,000,000, NHT 3%, Education Tax 2.25% after NIS.
  • Income tax: 25% above the tax-free amount, then 30% above J$6,000,000.
  • The tax-free amount for the whole of 2026 is J$1,876,614 (the rise to J$1,902,360 starts in April).

How the result is worked out

Start from your profit: sales minus your costs. NIS is 6% of profit up to J$5,000,000 and is taken off before income tax. Education Tax is 2.25% of profit after NIS. NHT is 3% of profit. Income tax is 25% of what remains above the tax-free amount, up to J$6,000,000, and 30% above that.

Frequently asked questions

How much tax does a self-employed person pay in Jamaica?

It depends on profit. On J$3,000,000 of profit, about J$569,296 goes to income tax, NIS, NHT and Education Tax together in 2026. Use the calculator above for your own figure.

Which tax-free amount applies in 2026?

The yearly amount rose to J$1,902,360 on 1 April 2026. Because the rise starts in April, the effective amount for the whole of 2026 is J$1,876,614, according to TAJ.

Is this calculator tax advice?

No. It is an estimate for planning. Confirm the exact amounts with TAJ.
