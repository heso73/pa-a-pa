NIS calculator for Jamaica

The National Insurance Scheme (NIS) takes a share of your pay and gives you access to a pension and other benefits. Enter your monthly pay and see the contribution for an employee, an employer or a self-employed person.

In short
  • Employee 3%, employer 3%, self-employed 6%.
  • Contributions stop at a yearly ceiling of J$5,000,000 of pay (about J$416,667 a month).
  • NIS is taken off before income tax and Education Tax are worked out.

Frequently asked questions

What is the NIS rate in Jamaica?

Employees and employers each pay 3% of pay, up to J$5,000,000 a year. Self-employed people pay 6% of profit up to the same ceiling.

What is the NIS ceiling in 2026?

J$5,000,000 of pay a year, which is about J$416,667 a month.

Is NIS deducted before income tax?

Yes. NIS is taken off before income tax and Education Tax are worked out.
