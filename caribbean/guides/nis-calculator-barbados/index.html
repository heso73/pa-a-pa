National Insurance (NIS) calculator for Barbados

National Insurance in Barbados also funds health service, unemployment and training. Enter a monthly pay and see the contribution for an employee, an employer or a self-employed person.

In short
  • Private-sector employee 11%, employer 12.75%, self-employed 17%, on earnings up to BDS$5,360 a month in 2026 (BDS$5,450 in 2027).
  • Resilience and Regeneration Fund: 0.25% of gross pay, paid by employees, employers and the self-employed, with no ceiling.
  • The fund rose from 0.1% to 0.25% for employees on 1 April 2025.

Frequently asked questions

What is the National Insurance rate in Barbados?

For a private-sector employee, 11% of insurable earnings, and 12.75% for the employer. Self-employed people pay 17%. A 0.25% Resilience and Regeneration Fund is added on gross earnings.

What is the NIS ceiling in Barbados in 2026?

BDS$5,360 a month, or BDS$1,238 a week. It is BDS$5,450 a month from 2027.

Is National Insurance tax deductible in Barbados?

No. The BRA says National Insurance paid by an employee or a self-employed person is not tax deductible.
