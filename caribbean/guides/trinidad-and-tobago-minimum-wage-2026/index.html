Minimum wage in Trinidad and Tobago in 2026

The national minimum wage in Trinidad and Tobago is TT$20.50 an hour. Here is what that is per week and per month, the overtime rate, and what a minimum-wage worker takes home after National Insurance and the Health Surcharge.

In short
  • TT$20.50 an hour since 1 January 2024, up from TT$17.50.
  • TT$820 for a 40-hour week and about TT$3,553 a month before deductions.
  • No income tax is due at this level, because the pay is below the TT$90,000 personal allowance. About TT$3,326 a month reaches the worker.

The rate comes from the Minimum Wages (Amendment) Order 2023 (Legal Notice No. 390 of 2023), as listed by WageIndicator. It applies to every sector. The 2027 Budget presented on 12 October 2026 did not announce a new national rate in the commentaries we read; if the Minimum Wages Board changes it, this page will be updated.

What a minimum-wage worker takes home

A worker paid TT$20.50 for 40 hours a week earns about TT$3,553.33 a month. That is below the personal allowance of TT$90,000 a year, so no PAYE is due. The worker still pays National Insurance (about TT$191.88, at the 2026 employee share of 5.4%) and the Health Surcharge (about TT$35.75, TT$8.25 a week). About TT$3,325.70 a month is left.

Frequently asked questions

What is the minimum wage in Trinidad and Tobago in 2026?

TT$20.50 an hour, in force since 1 January 2024. It is the same in every sector.

How much is the minimum wage per month in Trinidad and Tobago?

About TT$3,553.33 a month before deductions, for 40 hours a week over 52 weeks divided by 12.

What is the overtime rate?

Time and a half is the usual rule, which is TT$30.75 an hour at the minimum wage. Confirm your case with the Ministry of Labour.

Does a minimum-wage worker pay income tax?

No. The yearly pay of TT$42,640 is below the TT$90,000 personal allowance. The worker still pays National Insurance and the Health Surcharge.
