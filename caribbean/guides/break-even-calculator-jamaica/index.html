Break-even calculator for a small business in Jamaica

Your break-even point is the number of items you must sell each month before you stop losing money. Enter your fixed costs, your price and what each item costs you, and the calculator does the rest.

In short
  • Break-even = fixed costs per month divided by the margin per item.
  • Margin per item = selling price minus what the item costs you.
  • Put your own pay in the fixed costs, or you will break even but still not eat.

Frequently asked questions

What is a break-even point?

The sales level at which your income exactly covers your costs. Below it you lose money, above it you make a profit.

How do I calculate break-even?

Divide your monthly fixed costs by the margin on each item, which is the selling price minus the cost of the item.

Does break-even include tax?

No. It is before income tax and contributions on your own profit.
