Minimum wage in Barbados in 2026

Since 21 January 2026 the national minimum wage in Barbados is BDS$10.71 an hour. Here is what that is per week and per month, the overtime rate, and what a minimum-wage worker takes home.

In short
  • BDS$10.71 an hour from 21 January 2026, up from BDS$10.50 (and BDS$8.50 before June 2025).
  • BDS$428.40 for a 40-hour week and about BDS$1,856 a month before deductions. Overtime is paid at 150%.
  • No income tax is due at this level, because the pay is below the BDS$25,000 allowance. About BDS$1,648 a month reaches the worker.

What a minimum-wage worker takes home

A worker paid BDS$10.71 for 40 hours a week earns about BDS$1,856.40 a month. That is below the BDS$25,000 yearly allowance, so no income tax is due. The worker still pays National Insurance and related contributions at 11% (about BDS$204.20) and the Resilience and Regeneration Fund at 0.25% (about BDS$4.64). About BDS$1,647.56 a month is left.

Frequently asked questions

What is the minimum wage in Barbados in 2026?

BDS$10.71 an hour from 21 January 2026 for all workers except security guards, who earn at least BDS$11.66 an hour.

How much is the minimum wage per month in Barbados?

About BDS$1,856.40 a month before deductions, for 40 hours a week over 52 weeks divided by 12.

What is the overtime rate in Barbados?

At least 150% of the ordinary rate, which is about BDS$16.07 an hour at the minimum wage.

Does a minimum-wage worker pay income tax in Barbados?

No. The yearly pay of about BDS$22,277 is below the BDS$25,000 allowance. National Insurance and the Resilience and Regeneration Fund are still deducted.
