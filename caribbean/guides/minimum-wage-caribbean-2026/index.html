Minimum wage in the Caribbean in 2026

There is no single Caribbean minimum wage: each country sets its own, in its own currency. Here are the 2026 rates for the four countries Pa a Pa covers in detail, with the monthly equivalent and a link to each full guide.

In short
  • Jamaica: J$17,000 for a 40-hour week (J$425 an hour) from 1 July 2026.
  • Trinidad and Tobago: TT$20.50 an hour since 1 January 2024.
  • Barbados: BDS$10.71 an hour since 21 January 2026.
  • Dominican Republic: from RD$16,993.20 to RD$29,988.00 a month depending on company size, from 1 February 2026.

Monthly amounts are for 40 hours a week over 52 weeks divided by 12, before deductions, except the Dominican Republic, where the rates are monthly figures. The currencies are different (the Jamaican, Trinidad and Tobago, Barbados and Dominican pesos and dollars are not worth the same), so the numbers cannot be compared directly without converting them.

Overtime and take-home pay

Jamaica: J$637.50 an hour for hours beyond 40 in a week and J$850 for double time. Barbados: 150% of the ordinary rate, which is BDS$16.07 an hour at the minimum wage. Trinidad and Tobago: time and a half is the usual rule, TT$30.75 an hour at the minimum wage. At these pay levels income tax is not due in Jamaica, Trinidad and Tobago or Barbados, but social contributions are still deducted. Figures last checked on 12 November 2026 against each country's tax or labour authority (Jamaica: Ministry of Labour and Social Security; Trinidad and Tobago: Ministry of Labour and WageIndicator; Barbados: Ministry of Labour notice; Dominican Republic: DGII and the Comité Nacional de Salarios, resolution CNS-01-2025). Confirm the current amount with the authority of your country before you pay.

Frequently asked questions

What is the minimum wage in the Caribbean?

There is no regional rate. In 2026 it is J$17,000 a week in Jamaica, TT$20.50 an hour in Trinidad and Tobago, BDS$10.71 an hour in Barbados, and RD$16,993.20 to RD$29,988.00 a month in the Dominican Republic, depending on company size.

Which Caribbean country has the highest minimum wage?

The rates are in different currencies, so they cannot be ranked by the number alone. Convert them to one currency at the current exchange rate to compare them.

Is the minimum wage the same for every worker?

Not always. Barbados and Jamaica have a higher rate for security guards, and the Dominican Republic sets the rate by company size.
