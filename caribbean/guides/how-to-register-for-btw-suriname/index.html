How to register for BTW (VAT) in Suriname

Suriname replaced its old turnover tax with a 10% VAT (BTW) on 1 January 2023. Anyone who runs a business independently in Suriname is covered by the law.

In short
  • The standard BTW rate is 10%, in force since 1 January 2023. Listed luxury goods pay 25%.
  • The law defines an entrepreneur as anyone who independently carries on a business or profession in Suriname.
  • You register with the Belastingdienst and get a tax number (FIN).
  • Returns are filed monthly. Check the exact deadline and any small-business exemption with the Belastingdienst, because sources differ.

Who is covered

The BTW law taxes goods and services supplied for payment in Suriname by an entrepreneur, and imports. An entrepreneur is anyone who independently carries on a business or profession in Suriname.

Steps

  1. Register your business (Chamber of Commerce) first.
  2. Register with the Belastingdienst for BTW and get your tax identification number (FIN).
  3. Charge 10% on taxable sales and show it on the invoice.
  4. File your return monthly and pay with it.

What to confirm

Sources disagree on whether small businesses below a yearly turnover threshold are exempt from registering, and on the exact deadline in the month. The BTW Act 2022 and the Belastingdienst website are the reference: ask them before you decide not to register.

Information verified on 21 November 2026 from the BTW Act 2022 (S.B. 2022 No. 121) and tax summaries. The registration threshold is not confirmed here: check with the Belastingdienst.

Frequently asked questions

What is the BTW rate in Suriname?

10% standard since 1 January 2023, with 25% on listed luxury goods.

Who has to charge BTW?

Entrepreneurs: anyone who independently carries on a business or profession in Suriname.

How often do I file?

Monthly.

Is there a small-business exemption?

Some sources mention one below a yearly turnover level. Confirm the current rule with the Belastingdienst.

What is the FIN?

The tax identification number assigned when you register.
