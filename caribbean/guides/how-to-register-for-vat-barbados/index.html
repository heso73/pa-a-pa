How to register for VAT in Barbados

Barbados charges VAT at 17.5% on most goods and services. You register with the Barbados Revenue Authority (BRA), online.

In short
  • The BRA lists the standard VAT rate at 17.5%, with 7.5% on direct tourism accommodation services, 22% on telecommunications and 0% on zero-rated items.
  • The BRA says you must register if your annual income is BDS$200,000 or more (monthly revenue above $16,666.67).
  • Registration is completed online in the TAMIS portal. A single registration form also covers the TIN, small business licence, customs and NIS.
  • The March 2026 budget reportedly raised the level from 1 October 2026, but the BRA pages we found still show BDS$200,000: confirm with the BRA.

Who must register

The BRA says these persons must register for VAT: those whose annual income is $200,000 or more, those whose monthly revenue is above $16,666.67, and promoters of public entertainment. A "person" can be an individual, a company, a partnership, a club, a cooperative or another body.

How to register

  1. Register your business name (individuals and partnerships) or your company first, with the Corporate Affairs office.
  2. Register for tax online in the BRA's TAMIS portal.
  3. Or use the single registration form (singleregistration.gov.bb), which also covers the small business licence, customs and NIS.
  4. Charge VAT on your taxable sales and file your returns in TAMIS.

The threshold: confirm it

The BRA pages we found show BDS$200,000 per year as the current level. Budget coverage in 2026 mentioned an increase from 1 October 2026. Ask the BRA which level applies to you before you decide not to register.

Information verified on 21 November 2026 on the Barbados Revenue Authority website (bra.gov.bb): VAT rates, who must register, TAMIS registration, single registration portal.

Frequently asked questions

What is the VAT rate in Barbados?

17.5% for standard supplies, according to the BRA, with 7.5% on direct tourism accommodation services and 22% on telecommunications.

What is the VAT registration threshold in Barbados?

The BRA lists BDS$200,000 a year ($16,666.67 a month). A 2026 budget increase was reported but is not shown on the BRA pages we found: confirm with the BRA.

Where do I register?

Online in the BRA TAMIS portal, or through the single registration form.

Do I need to register if I promote public entertainment?

Yes: the BRA lists promoters of public entertainment as required to register for VAT.
