VAT calculator for Trinidad and Tobago

Value Added Tax (VAT) in Trinidad and Tobago is 12.5%. Enter an amount to add the VAT to a price, or to find the price before VAT inside a total.

In short
  • Standard VAT rate: 12.5%.
  • You must register if you sell TT$500,000 or more in a twelve-month period, or expect to. Registration is free.
  • Digital services from abroad become subject to 12.5% VAT from 1 March 2027 (2027 Budget proposal).

How it works

To add VAT, multiply the price by 1.125. To take VAT out of a total, divide it by 1.125. On TT$1,125 the VAT is TT$125 and the price before VAT is TT$1,000.

Most small businesses start below the registration threshold and do not charge VAT yet. The Ministry of Finance says individuals and businesses must register when they sell TT$500,000 or more in twelve months, or forecast that. Registered businesses file VAT returns and pay the Inland Revenue Division; your filing period is set when you register.

What is changing

The 2025 Budget removed VAT from a wide range of basic food items from 17 October 2025 and announced a review of VAT with a possible move to a sales tax; no date has been set. The 2027 Budget proposes VAT on digital and remote services supplied by non-resident providers from 1 March 2027. Figures last checked on 12 November 2026 against the Ministry of Finance, the Inland Revenue Division (IRD), the National Insurance Board (NIBTT), the PwC Worldwide Tax Summaries and the 2027 Budget commentaries. Confirm the current amount with the IRD or your payroll provider before you pay.

Remember

Some goods and services are zero-rated or exempt, so 12.5% does not apply to everything. Check your item with the IRD before you charge or claim VAT.

Frequently asked questions

What is the VAT rate in Trinidad and Tobago?

12.5%, according to the Ministry of Finance.

How do I take VAT out of a price?

Divide the VAT-inclusive total by 1.125. The difference is the VAT.

When must a business register for VAT in Trinidad and Tobago?

When it sells TT$500,000 or more in a twelve-month period, or forecasts sales of that amount. Registration is free.

Will VAT be replaced by a sales tax?

The government has said it is reviewing the VAT system with a possible move to a sales tax, but no date or rate has been announced.
