Starting a business in Barbados: taxes, registration and steps

A plain-words guide for first-time entrepreneurs in Barbados: what you register, what you pay and when. Figures are 2026 estimates for planning. Barbados Revenue Authority (BRA) gives you the exact amounts when you register.

Steps to start

  1. Get your TIN (Taxpayer Identification Number) from the BRA
    A number that identifies you to the tax office. You need it for almost every other step.
  2. Choose your business name and check that it is free
    If you trade under any name other than your own, you must register it. Reserve it with CAIPO first.
  3. Register the business name, or incorporate the company, with CAIPO
    Online or in person. For a business name, approval usually takes about five working days.
  4. Register with the National Insurance Office (NIS)
    Self-employed people must register and contribute. It gives access to a pension and other benefits.
  5. Register with the BRA and plan your advance tax payments
    Self-employed people pay income tax in advance, each quarter, and file a yearly return by April 30.
  6. Open a separate bank account for the business
    Keeping business money apart from household money makes your numbers clearer and your taxes easier to pay.
  7. Register as an employer and prepare monthly payments (if you hire employees)
    With employees you pay income tax (PAYE) and NIS each month, by the 15th of the following month.
  8. Register for VAT (when your sales reach the threshold)
    Required once your taxable sales pass the VAT threshold. Register with the BRA.

Choosing a legal form

  • Registered business name: You run the business as an individual under your own name or a business name. You and the business are the same person for tax. Usually the simplest way to start.
  • Limited company: The company is its own legal person, separate from you. It costs more to set up and has more paperwork, but it can limit your personal risk. A company costs more to set up than a business name. The registry will quote you the current fees.

Taxes and contributions

  • Income tax: 11.5% on the first BDS$50,000 above the allowance, then 27.5% (personal allowance per year).
  • NIS and related funds (17%): National Insurance and related funds (pension, health, training). Self-employed people pay 17% of insurable earnings, up to BDS$5,360 a month.
  • Resilience and Regeneration Fund (0.25%): A fund for climate and disaster resilience. It applies to all earnings, with no ceiling.
  • Value Added Tax (VAT): 17.5%. Plan to register once your sales reach BDS$200,000. A 2026 budget measure may raise the level to BDS$350,000 from 1 October 2026: the BRA gives you the current level when you register. Your filing period is set when you register.

Tax calendar

  • March 15, June 15, September 15 and December 15: Pay your advance income tax instalment. NIS for the self-employed follows its own schedule: ask the National Insurance Office.
  • By April 30: File your income tax return for the year before.
  • Every month: Every month: pay income tax (PAYE) and NIS for the previous month.

Frequently asked questions

How much tax does a self-employed person pay in Barbados?

On profit: 11.5% on the first BDS$50,000 above the allowance, then 27.5% (personal allowance per year). Contributions: NIS and related funds 17% (up to BDS$5,360 a month), Resilience and Regeneration Fund 0.25%. Example: with BDS$12,000 of sales a month, about BDS$4,182 a month is left after tax.

Do I need to register for VAT in Barbados?

Value Added Tax (VAT) 17.5%; register from BDS$200,000 in yearly sales. Plan to register once your sales reach BDS$200,000. A 2026 budget measure may raise the level to BDS$350,000 from 1 October 2026: the BRA gives you the current level when you register. Your filing period is set when you register.

Where do I register a business in Barbados?

With Corporate Affairs and Intellectual Property Office (CAIPO) and Barbados Revenue Authority (BRA). First steps: Get your TIN (Taxpayer Identification Number) from the BRA; Choose your business name and check that it is free; Register the business name, or incorporate the company, with CAIPO.

How much does it cost to employ someone in Barbados?

On top of gross pay, the employer adds: NIS and related levies 12.75%, Resilience and Regeneration Fund 0.25%. Every month: pay income tax (PAYE) and NIS for the previous month.
