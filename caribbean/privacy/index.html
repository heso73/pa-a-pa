Privacy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Your answers stay on your device

The test, your numbers and your report are stored only in your browser. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.

Partner links

When you tap a partner link, we count the click per partner, without cookies. No name or e-mail is attached to it. Some partner links are affiliate links: if you sign up, the partner may pay us a commission, at no extra cost to you.

Founder tips by e-mail (optional)

At the bottom of the app you can choose to receive founder tips by e-mail. This is the only case where we receive personal data: the e-mail address you enter yourself in the form. The app works fully without it.

  • The form loads only after you tap “I want to get tips”. Until then nothing is sent to Brevo.
  • Addresses are handled by Brevo, the e-mail service we use. You receive a welcome e-mail when you subscribe.
  • We use your address only to send these tips. It is never sold or passed on.
  • Every e-mail has an unsubscribe link. To access, correct or delete your data, write to contact@caribbeanmetadata.org.

Services

The app is hosted on GitHub Pages. Visits are counted with GoatCounter (no cookies).
