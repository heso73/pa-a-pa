Privacy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Your answers stay on your device

The test, your numbers and your report are stored only in your browser. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.

E-mail (optional)

If you subscribe to the weekly tip, we store your e-mail address, your country, your language and the date. We use it only to send you tips and updates about your country’s figures. You can ask us to delete it at any time at contact@caribbeanmetadata.org.

Partner links

When you tap a partner link, we count the click (partner, country, language). No name or e-mail is attached to it.

Services

The app is hosted on GitHub Pages. Visits are counted with GoatCounter (no cookies). Subscriber data is stored with Supabase. Editor: Caribbean Metadata.
