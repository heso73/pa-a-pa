Pa a Pa Caribbean: test your business idea

Pa a Pa Caribbean is a free web app for first-time entrepreneurs. Enter your sales, your costs and what you need to live on, and it estimates your taxes and social contributions, what is left for you each month, and whether the project can pay your bills. You also get a strengths and risks report, a launch checklist, a tax calendar and a step-by-step coach. No sign-up: your answers stay on your device.
