Jamaica Self-Employed Starter Kit

One Excel workbook to run a one-person business in Jamaica: log what you earn and spend, see what to set aside for taxes, and know when GCT registration is coming.

What you get (one .xlsx file, works in Excel and Google Sheets)
  • Income and Expenses logs: one line per invoice or cost, 200 rows each.
  • Tax estimate: income tax (25% / 30%), NIS (6%), NHT (2%) and Education Tax (2.25%), worked out from your own figures.
  • Quarterly sheet: the amount to set aside for 15 March, 15 June, 15 September and 15 December, with a tracker of what you have paid.
  • GCT watch: your rolling 12-month sales against the J$15,000,000 registration threshold, with a warning at 80%.
  • Invoice template: TRN, optional GCT, totals calculated for you. Print it or save it as PDF.
  • Start-up checklist: TRN, business name, NIS, NHT, quarterly payments, GCT, in order, with where to go and what to bring.

Built on the official figures

Rates and thresholds come from Tax Administration Jamaica and the Companies Office of Jamaica, and each sheet says where. The 2026 tax-free threshold is J$1,876,614 for the calendar year (J$1,902,360 from 1 April 2026). Every rate sits on one Rates sheet, so when TAJ changes a figure you change one cell and the whole workbook follows.

What it is not

It is a planning tool, not tax advice. The estimate is simplified: it leaves out pensions, credits and other deductions. Check the final amounts with TAJ or an accountant before you pay or file. There are no macros, no external links and nothing is sent anywhere: your figures stay in your file.

Questions

Does it work in Google Sheets?

Yes. Upload the .xlsx to Google Drive and open it with Google Sheets, or use Excel on any device.

Is it up to date for 2026?

Yes. The tax-free threshold of J$1,876,614 for 2026 and the J$15,000,000 GCT threshold were checked against TAJ and JIS in November 2026. The Rates sheet lets you update any figure.

I am not registered for GCT. Can I still use the invoice?

Yes. Leave the GCT column empty and write n/a on the GCT registration line.
